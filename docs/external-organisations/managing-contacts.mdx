# Managing Contacts

Each external organisation can have one or more contact people — the individuals at that organisation who are your actual points of contact for <Term path="referral" plural /> and co-ordination.

## Contacts tab

Open an organisation's detail page. The **Contacts** tab shows all contacts linked to that organisation, including their name, role or title, phone number, and email address.

## Adding a contact

Click **Add Contact** in the Contacts tab. Fill in the following details:

- **First name** and **Last name** (both required)
- **Position / Title** — for example, Social Worker
- **Phone**
- **Email**

Click **Add Contact** to add the contact. They will appear immediately as a selectable referrer source when creating entries.

## Editing and removing contacts

Use the **edit icon** on any contact row to update their details.

You cannot remove a contact from this screen. If someone has left the organisation, edit their details or ask your administrator.

## Using contacts in entries

When creating an entry (<Term path="referral" />), the **Referrer contact** field searches the contacts across all your external organisations at once. Keeping contacts up to date ensures that <Term path="referral" /> attribution is accurate and your team can follow up with the right people.
