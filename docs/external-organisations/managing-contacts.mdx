# Managing Contacts

Each external organisation can have one or more contact people — the individuals at that organisation who are your actual points of contact for <Term path="referral" plural /> and co-ordination.

## Contacts tab

Open an organisation's detail page. The **Contacts** tab shows all contacts linked to that organisation, including their name, role or title, phone number, and email address.

## Adding a contact

Click **Add Contact** in the Contacts tab. Fill in the following details:

- **Full name** (required)
- **Role or job title** — for example, Social Worker, Principal, or GP
- **Phone number**
- **Email address**

Click **Save** to add the contact. They will appear immediately as a selectable referrer source when creating entries.

## Editing and removing contacts

Use the **edit icon** on any contact row to update their details.

Contacts can be removed if they are no longer at the organisation. Removing a contact will not affect historical records where they were previously referenced — those records keep the name and details as they were at the time.

## Using contacts in entries

When creating an entry (<Term path="referral" />), the **Referrer Source** field pulls from the contacts list across all your external organisations. Keeping contacts up to date ensures that <Term path="referral" /> attribution is accurate and your team can follow up with the right people.
