# Managing Contacts

Each external organisation can have one or more contact people — the individuals at that organisation who are your actual points of contact for <Term path="referral" plural /> and co-ordination.

## Contacts tab

Open an organisation's detail page. The **Contacts** tab lists everyone linked to that organisation, in three columns — **Name**, **Phone** and **Email**. Their role or job title sits in smaller text under the name rather than in a column of its own.

## Adding a contact

Click **Add Contact** in the Contacts tab. Fill in the following details:

- **First name** (required)
- **Last name** (required)
- **Role or job title** — for example, Social Worker, Principal, or GP
- **Phone number**
- **Email address**

Click **Add Contact** to save them. They will appear immediately as a selectable referrer source when creating entries.

## Editing contacts

Use the **edit icon** — the pencil on any contact row — to update their details. **Save changes** commits them.

<Callout color="amber" title="There is no way to remove a contact">
The contacts list has no delete action. If somebody has left the organisation, edit their entry to say so, or add their replacement alongside them.

This is deliberate as far as records go: entries that named that person keep the name and details as they were at the time, so removing them would not tidy the history anyway.
</Callout>

## Using contacts in entries

When creating an entry (<Term path="referral" />), the **Referrer Source** field pulls from the contacts list across all your external organisations. Keeping contacts up to date ensures that <Term path="referral" /> attribution is accurate and your team can follow up with the right people.
