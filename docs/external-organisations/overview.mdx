# External Organisations

The Organisations section lists partner and referrer organisations — the external agencies, GPs, schools, iwi services, and community groups that refer people to your organisation or that you collaborate with.

:::note Access
The Organisations section is only visible to users with the Org Management or Admin role. If you don't see "Organisations" in the sidebar, contact your administrator.
:::

## What are external organisations?

External organisations are entities outside your own organisation that interact with your work — they send <Term path="referral" plural />, receive <Term path="referral" plural />, or provide support alongside you.

Examples include:

- A GP practice that refers whānau into your service
- A school that flags a student's needs
- A housing provider you co-ordinate care alongside
- An iwi service that shares <Term path="serviceUser" plural /> across programmes

Keeping this list accurate ensures:

- <Term path="referral" /> sources are correctly attributed when creating entries
- Contact people can be linked to incoming <Term path="referral" plural />
- Your team has current contact details for co-ordination

## The organisations list

The list has two tabs:

- **Active** — organisations currently working with you
- **Inactive** — organisations that have been deactivated (they remain in the system for historical records)

You can filter by **organisation type** and search by name to find a specific organisation quickly.

## Creating a new organisation

Click **Add Organisation** (top right of the list). Fill in the following details:

- **Organisation name** (required)
- **Organisation type** — for example, Health, Education, Social Services, or Iwi/Community
- **Contact details** — address, phone number, and website

Click **Save** to add the organisation. It will appear in the Active tab straight away.

## Editing an organisation

Click an organisation row to open the detail view. Use the **Edit** button in the Details tab to update any information.

:::tip
See [Managing Contacts](./managing-contacts) to add or update individual contact people at an organisation.
:::
