## The shape of the form

The <Term path="referral" /> form is a **three-step wizard**:

| Step | Name | Covers |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | **Intake** | Client & referral |
| 2 | **Consent** | Permissions on file |
| 3 | **Review & Submit** | Confirm & dispatch |

<Callout color="sage" title="The record is created at the end of Step 1">
Finish the first step and click **Continue**, and the <Term path="referral" /> exists — a part-finished one is never lost. Before that point, leaving the page loses what you typed.
</Callout>
