# <Term path="referral" /> Details

These fields make up the rest of **Step 1 (Intake)**, alongside [selecting the client](/referrals/client-information).

## Service

Select the **service contract** the <Term path="referral" /> is for. Do this early — it determines which service-specific questions appear further down the page, and which team will pick the <Term path="referral" /> up.

## The core fields

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Referral type** | Yes | Self, Whānau, Educational or Professional. Changes which referrer fields you are asked for. |
| **Urgency level** | Yes | Routine, Priority, Urgent, Crisis or Emergency. |
| **Referral date** | Yes | Cannot be in the future. |
| **Channel** | Yes | How the <Term path="referral" /> reached you. |
| **Reason for referral** | Yes | Minimum 10 characters, maximum 2000. |
| **Additional referral details** | No | Free text, in the collapsible section below the reason. |

### Channel options

Channel records how the <Term path="referral" /> arrived:

Internal · Face to Face · Walk-in · Email · Electronic · Event · Network

### Urgency levels

The form shows the expected response time beside each level as you choose it:

| Level | Response time | Use when |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| **Emergency** | Now | Immediate safety concern requiring emergency response |
| **Crisis** | 4 hours | Crisis situation, handle urgently |
| **Urgent** | 24 hours | Needs attention within a day |
| **Priority** | 3 days | Important but not an emergency |
| **Routine** | 5 days | Standard processing |

Urgency drives the priority badge on dashboards and the **Needs Attention** count on the Intake Officer dashboard, so it has a direct effect on whether the <Term path="referral" /> gets picked up quickly.

## Who made the referral

The referrer fields appear once you choose a **referral type**:

| Referral type | What you provide |
|---------------|------------------|
| **Self** | Nothing — the client referred themselves |
| **Whānau** | Select the whānau member making the <Term path="referral" />. If the client is under 16, a contact phone number is also required. |
| **Educational** | Search for the school or organisation, then select or add the contact person there |
| **Professional** | Search for the organisation, then select or add the contact person there |

For Educational and Professional <Term path="referral" plural />, if the contact you need is not listed you can add them without leaving the wizard.

<Callout color="teal" title="School name fills itself in">
On an **Educational** <Term path="referral" />, selecting the organisation automatically fills in the school name field for you.
</Callout>

## Service-specific questions

Below the standard fields you may see an **Entry Details** panel containing extra questions belonging to the service you selected — for example child safety concerns or risk ratings. These vary by service.

<Callout color="amber" title="These will not stop you continuing">
Service-specific questions are advisory at intake. Complete as much as you can — you can come back and finish them later, and leaving them blank does not block the **Continue** button.
</Callout>

## Continuing

**Continue** becomes available once you have selected a client and a service. Clicking it creates the <Term path="referral" /> and moves you to the Consent step.
