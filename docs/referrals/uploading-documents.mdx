# Uploading <Term path="referral" /> Documents

Supporting documents — most commonly a signed consent form — can be attached to a <Term path="referral" /> as part of the intake process.

## Where to upload

Document upload sits in a collapsible section beneath the consent form on **Step 2 (Consent)** of the <Term path="referral" /> wizard.

<Callout color="teal" title="You need to have completed Step 1 first">
Documents attach to a <Term path="referral" /> record, and that record is only created when you click **Continue** at the end of Step 1. If you have not got past Step 1 yet, there is nothing to attach the file to.
</Callout>

## How to upload

1. Open the documents section on the Consent step
2. Drag a file onto the drop zone, or click it to browse
3. Wait for the upload to confirm before leaving the page

## Accepted file types

| Category | Formats |
|----------|---------|
| **Documents** | PDF, Word (`.doc`, `.docx`), Excel (`.xls`, `.xlsx`), PowerPoint (`.ppt`, `.pptx`) |
| **Text** | `.txt`, `.csv` |
| **Images** | `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.gif`, `.webp`, `.svg` |

**Maximum file size: 10 MB per file.** Files that are too large, or of a type not listed above, are rejected with a message explaining why.

<Callout color="amber" title="Scans and photos">
A photographed or scanned consent form is fine — images are accepted. If a scan comes out larger than 10 MB, reduce the resolution or save it as a PDF rather than splitting it into multiple files.
</Callout>

## Signed consent documents

The signed consent form is tracked separately from the consent checkboxes on the form. Ticking the consent boxes records *that* consent was obtained; uploading the document provides the evidence.

If no signed consent document has been uploaded by the time you dispatch, the dispatch dialog shows a reminder. This does **not** block dispatch — but the document stays outstanding against the <Term path="referral" /> until it is provided.
