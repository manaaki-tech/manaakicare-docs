# Document Library

The Templates section is a shared document library for your organisation. It stores forms, consent templates, policy documents, and other files that staff reference or share with whānau.

:::note Access
All authenticated staff can view and download documents. Uploading and deleting documents is typically limited to administrators and programme managers.
:::

## What is the document library?

The document library holds files that belong to your organisation rather than to a specific person or referral. Think of it as your organisation's shared filing cabinet for:

- Blank consent forms
- Assessment templates
- Service brochures
- Policy documents
- Referral forms from external partners

Documents can be scoped to your whole organisation or to a specific service. Use the filter to narrow the view to documents relevant to your work.

:::note
Documents attached to individual referrals or service episodes are managed within those records, not here. The Templates library is for shared organisation-level files.
:::

## Viewing and downloading documents

Browse the list, use the search bar to find a document by name, or filter by service. Click the **download icon** on any row to save the file to your device.

## Uploading a document

Click **Upload Document** (top right). Then:

1. Select the file from your device
2. Give it a clear, descriptive name
3. Choose the service scope — or leave it as organisation-wide if it applies across all services
4. Click **Upload**

Supported file types include PDF, Word, Excel, and common image formats.

## Deleting a document

Use the **delete icon** on a document row to remove a file. Deletion is permanent — if the document may be needed for historical reference, consider leaving it in place rather than deleting it.
