# Document Library

The Templates section is a shared document library for your organisation. It stores forms, consent templates, policy documents, and other files that staff reference or share with whānau.

:::note Access
Everyone can view and download organisation-wide documents, and documents for the services they work in. Only administrators and Service Managers can upload and delete documents.
:::

## What is the document library?

The document library holds files that belong to your organisation rather than to a specific person or <Term path="referral" />. Think of it as your organisation's shared filing cabinet for:

- Blank consent forms
- Assessment templates
- Service brochures
- Policy documents
- <Term path="referral" /> forms from external partners

Documents can be scoped to your whole organisation or to a specific service. Use the filter to narrow the view to documents relevant to your work.

:::note
Documents attached to individual <Term path="referral" plural /> or <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> are managed within those records, not here. The Templates library is for shared organisation-level files.
:::

## Viewing and downloading documents

Browse the list, search by title or document type, or filter by service. Click **Download** on any row to save the file to your device.

## Uploading a document

Click **Upload Document** (top right). Then:

1. Drop a file or click to choose one
2. Check the **Title** (it is filled in from the file name)
3. Choose the **Scope**: leave it as **Organisation-wide (all services)**, or pick one service
4. Choose a **Document type**
5. Click **Upload**

Supported file types include PDF, Word, Excel, and common image formats.

## Deleting a document

Click the **…** menu on a document row and choose **Delete**, then confirm. You cannot undo this. If the document may be needed later, leave it in place.
