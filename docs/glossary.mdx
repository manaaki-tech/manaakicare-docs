# Glossary

Common terms and definitions used in Manaaki Central.

## A

**<Term path="activity" plural />**
: A record of work performed or interaction with a client during a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

**In-progress Journeys**
: The section of Manaaki Central showing <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that are currently underway. This is the default navigation label; your organisation may have renamed it.

**Assessment**
: A formal evaluation of client needs, strengths, and service requirements.

**Awareness and Entry**
: An example te reo Māori–informed navigation label for the Entries section, used by some NPO organisations to describe the point at which a person's needs first come to light and are recorded.

## C

**Care Journey**
: The full arc of support for a person from <Term path="referral" /> through to closure. In the application, a care journey is the detail view for a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, showing all related information, <Term path="activity" plural />, and compliance requirements in one workspace.

**<Term path="caseWorker" />**
: Front-line staff member who manages direct client relationships and service delivery.

**Client**
: An individual receiving services through Manaaki Central (also called a family member or <Term path="serviceUser" />).

**Completed Care**
: The section showing <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that have been closed or completed. Navigation label may be customised per organisation (e.g. "Preservation of Relationship").

**Compliance**
: The set of requirements that must be met during a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, as defined by the service contract or funding conditions. Manaaki Central tracks compliance through a panel in the Care Journey workspace.

## D

**Dynamic Fields**
: Service-specific form fields that appear in the entry wizard or care journey workspace based on the selected service. These are configured in the backend and vary by service type.

## E

**Entries**
: The Entries section (navigating to `/referrals`) is where new intake <Term path="referral" plural /> are created and managed through the early stages of the pipeline. Some organisations relabel this (e.g. "Awareness and Entry").

**Episode**
: See <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

**External Organisation**
: A partner agency, referrer, or collaborative organisation outside your own. Stored in the Organisations directory and used to attribute <Term path="referral" /> sources.

## F

**Family**
: A group of related individuals tracked together in the system.

**Family Member**
: An individual within a family unit (may or may not be receiving services).

## K

**Kaimahi**
: Te reo Māori: worker, staff member. Used in some places in Manaaki Central to refer to the allocated <Term path="caseWorker" /> on an <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

## P

**Preservation of Relationship**
: An example te reo Māori–informed navigation label for the Completed Care section, reflecting the view that closing a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> does not end the relationship with whānau.

## R

**<Term path="referral" plural />**
: The formal intake process for bringing a client into service, documenting needs and initiating service delivery.

**<Term path="referral" /> Source**
: The organisation or individual who referred the client for services.

## S

**<Term path="serviceEpisode" />**
: A time-bound period during which services are actively delivered to a client.

**<Term path="serviceUser" plural />**
: Another term for a client - an individual receiving services.

**Supervisor**
: A team lead who oversees <Term path="caseWorker" plural />, reviews work, and approves <Term path="referral" plural />.

## T

**Templates**
: The shared document library (`/organisation-documents`) for organisation-wide forms, consent templates, and policy documents.

## W

**Whānau**
: Te reo Māori: family, extended family. In Manaaki Central, whānau relationships are first-class data — family members can be linked to a <Term path="serviceUser" />'s record and tracked across the system.
