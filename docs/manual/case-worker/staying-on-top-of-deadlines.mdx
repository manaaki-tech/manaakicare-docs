# Staying on top of deadlines

**By the end of this page** you will know where to find what is due, and what
to do about it.

<PictureWords />

## The compliance panel

It sits down the right-hand side of someone's overview page. You may see only a
narrow bar there.

- **Hover** over the bar for a short summary, such as **3 of 5 on track**, with the
  counts of overdue, due soon and pending items.
- **Click** the bar to open the full panel as a sheet on the right, titled
  **Compliance**.
- Press **Pin panel** on the sheet to dock it on the page while you work. **Unpin
  panel** puts it back. On screens narrower than 1536 pixels, pinning collapses
  the side menu to make room.

Each line is a piece of paperwork your service requires. Next to it is when it
is due, and an **Upload** link for when you have it.

## Reading it

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| **due in 4 days** | You have time, but it is counting down |
| **due today** | Today |
| A locked padlock | A closure item. It stays locked until it is done, and it must be done (or overridden) before you can close the work |
| **0 of 4 on track** | How many of the required items are done. Closure items are not counted |

The items in the **Closure** group stay locked until they are done. You need them
when you close the work.

## Where the dates come from

Deadlines are counted from a fixed date, not from today. Depending on the item,
that is the date the <Term path="referral" /> was made, the date it was accepted
into your service, or the service start date.

<Callout color="amber" title="Check the start date before you start">
The start date is set when the <Term path="referral" /> is accepted into your
service. Clicking **Start working with...** later does not move it. So if you
start a fortnight after that date, deadlines counted from the start date may
already be due or overdue.

The start date can't be changed in the app. If it looks wrong, talk to your
supervisor before you click **Start working with...**.
</Callout>

## Uploading what is due

1. Click **Upload** on the line for the item. This opens the **Documents** tab.
2. Click **Upload**.
3. Choose the file, give it a **Title**, and pick the **Document type**.
4. Click **Upload**.

The line updates once the document is attached.

<Callout color="teal" title="Choose the document type carefully">
Pick the type that actually describes the document, not the closest thing on
the list. The type is how the system knows what it has been given — the file
name is not enough.

If you have uploaded something and the line still shows as outstanding, the
type you chose is the first thing to check.
</Callout>

## What happens if something is late

Day to day, nothing is blocked or switched off. You can keep working. The item
simply shows as outstanding.

It matters at the end. When you come to close the work, closure items still
missing are listed as blockers, and you have to write a justification before you
can finish. See [finishing up](/manual/case-worker/finishing-up).

Being late is a conversation, not a lockout. If a whānau will not sign
something, record what you have done and say so — that is a better record than
an empty line, and it is what you will want to point at when you write that
justification.

## If it does not look like this

- **The panel is not there** — you may be on the **Activities** tab, or this
  service has no requirements yet.
- **An item you expected is missing** — each service asks for different
  things.
- **You uploaded something but it still shows as due** — check the document
  type you chose. That is almost always the reason.
- **There is no Upload link** — the item is done, it is not a document, or the
  work has closed. You can still read the panel.
