# Finishing up

**By the end of this page** you will be able to close someone's work properly,
with the right reason and date recorded.

<PictureWords />

## Before you close anything

Closing is not the same as pausing. If you expect to pick the work back up —
they are in hospital, away, or out of contact for now — use **Put on Hold**
instead.

Close only when the work has genuinely ended.

<Callout color="teal" title="Check who does this in your team">
Whether you can close the work yourself, or only ask a manager to, depends on
your position on that particular service. Out of the box only supervisors and
managers close directly and everyone else requests approval — but each service
can be set up differently, so you may find you can close on one service and have
to ask on another.

Beyond that, many organisations expect the work to be taken to a supervisor
first as a matter of practice, whatever the system allows. Ask what is expected
where you work before closing something for the first time.
</Callout>

## Starting the closure

Open the person, find the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> card, and open its
actions menu.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/04-actions-menu.png"
  alt="The actions menu open, showing Put on Hold and Exit options"
  caption="Two options, and they are not alike. Putting the work on hold is easy to undo. Ending it is not."
  narrow
/>

Choose the option that ends the work. It is named after whatever your
organisation calls a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, so it may read **Exit**,
**Complete**, or something else — but it is the one that is not **Put on Hold**.

## The closure form

<Callout color="teal" title="You may be asking, not closing">
On some services, ending the work sends a request to a manager instead of
closing it straight away. If that applies to you, this form is headed **Request
<Term path="serviceEpisode" /> Exit**, it asks you to pick an approver, and the
button reads **Submit for Approval**. Everything else on this page still
applies. See [Manager approval for <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> exits](/releases/2026-09-12-exit-approval).
</Callout>

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/closure/01-close-the-journey.png"
  alt="The closure dialog, with a reason, an end date, a required exit summary, two checkboxes, and a list of outstanding requirements"
  caption="A few things to get right: why it ended, a summary of it, when it ended, and what to do about anything still outstanding."
  narrow
/>

The **Closure Reason**, **End Date**, and **Exit Summary** are all required —
the button at the bottom stays greyed out until you have filled them in, and it
tells you which one is missing.

<Callout color="terracotta" title="You cannot undo this">
Once the work is closed, its status is final. There is no button anywhere in
the system to reopen it or change it afterwards.

That is not a reason to avoid closing things — it is a reason to check the
reason and the date before you click.
</Callout>

### Why it ended

Pick the reason that is actually true.

Only **Service completed** records the work as genuinely finished. Every other
reason — moved out of area, left without notice, consent withdrawn, and the
rest — closes it without counting as a completion. Choosing "service completed"
for work that stopped because a whānau disengaged makes your organisation's
numbers meaningless.

Your organisation sets the list of reasons, so yours may differ from someone
else's. If a reason has a small **i** icon next to it, hover over it to read an
explanation of what that reason is for — useful when two of them sound similar.

### Exit summary

Right below the closure reason, write a short summary of how the work ended. The
form won't let you submit until you've written at least 10 characters, and a
counter under the box shows how you're doing. This replaces the old Exit Summary
Document upload; you no longer attach a separate file for it.

### When it ended

Put the real end date. If your last contact was three weeks ago, use that date.

It cannot be in the future.

### Anything still outstanding

If required paperwork is missing, the dialog says so in red and names each item —
*"2 requirements must be completed before closure"*, then the list: closure
checklist, evaluation form.

Going ahead anyway means turning on **Override** and **writing a justification**
before the closure button becomes available.

<Callout color="amber" title="The justification is not a formality">
Sometimes closing with paperwork missing is the right call — a whānau who has
moved on is not coming back to sign an evaluation form.

But what you type is kept, and someone will read it. Write the actual reason,
not "n/a".
</Callout>

### If you are ticking "consent withdrawn"

<Callout color="terracotta" title="This reaches further than the work you are closing">
Consent is withdrawn at the <Term path="referral" /> level, not just for this
piece of work. If the person has **other open
<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> under the same
<Term path="referral" />, those are closed too.**

The dialog will ask you for a reason and a confirmation that the person
actually requested it. Both are required, and the button changes to **Complete
& Withdraw Consent** so you know it is happening.

If you did not intend to end their other services, stop and choose a different
reason.
</Callout>

### Follow-up

If you tick **Follow-up required**, you must give a follow-up date. It can be
up to six months out.

## What closing does

- The <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is marked **Completed** or **Closed**, with
  your end date and reason recorded on it.
- The linked <Term path="referral" /> is set to match.
- An "Exit Episode" activity is written automatically, holding the reason and
  your exit summary. In that activity the summary appears under the heading
  **Closure Notes**, which is what the field used to be called.
- The record moves off your in-progress list and onto **Completed Care**.

Everything recorded stays. You can still open it and read the history — you
just cannot change it.

## If it does not look like this

- **There is no exit option** — either the work has already been closed, or you
  have an exit request waiting on a manager. The option disappears in both
  cases. Check the status on the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> card: **Awaiting
  Exit Review** means it's with your approver.
- **It will not let you pick an end date before the start date** — that is
  deliberate. If the start date is wrong, fix that first.
- **The closure documents are still locked** — they unlock once closure has
  begun. Start the closure, then upload them.
- **You closed it by mistake** — it cannot be reopened. Tell your supervisor
  straight away rather than quietly creating a duplicate record; how to handle
  it is their call.
