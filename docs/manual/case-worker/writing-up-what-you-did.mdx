# Writing up what you did

**By the end of this page** you will be able to record a visit, a phone call or
anything else you did, and use a template so you are not starting from a blank
box.

This is the thing you will do most often. It is worth ten minutes now to get
comfortable with it.

<PictureWords />

## Where the write-ups live

Open the person, then click the **Activities** tab.

## Recording something

Click **+ Log activity** — or use the form already open on the Activities tab.

### The essentials

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/activities/03-essentials.png"
  alt="The essentials tab of the activity form, with type, date, duration and people fields"
  caption="Five things: what kind of contact it was, when, how long, who was there, and where."
/>

Work down the form:

1. **What kind of activity** it was — a visit, a phone call, coordination with
   another agency, and so on.
2. **The date and time** it happened. Not today's date, unless it was today.
3. **How long** it took. This matters for funding reports, so a rough honest
   figure beats leaving it blank.
4. **Who was there** — pick from the people already linked to this person.
5. **Where** it happened.

### Was it in person or not?

If you choose **Remote**, an extra row appears asking how you made contact.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/activities/04-remote.png"
  alt="The remote option selected, revealing a contact type row"
  caption="Phone, video, text — say which. It is part of the record of what actually happened."
  narrow
/>

<Callout color="amber" title="Say honestly whether you actually saw them">
A phone call is not a visit, and recording it as one distorts the picture of
what a whānau has actually received. It also puts your organisation's reporting
wrong.
</Callout>

## Using a template

If you keep writing the same shape of note, use a template instead of starting
from nothing.

Click **Template** and choose one.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/activities/06-templates.png"
  alt="The template picker with four numbered steps, from the Template button through to appending it to the notes"
  caption="The preview on the left shows what you will get. It drops the headings in — you fill in the rest."
/>

1. **Template** — opens the list.
2. **The list** — templates set up for this service come first, then your
   organisation's. Search if there are a lot of them.
3. **Preview** — shows what that template will put in, before you commit to it.
4. **Append to notes** — drops the headings into your notes. You fill in the
   rest underneath.

It *adds to* whatever you have already written rather than replacing it, so you
can pick a template after you have started.

A template only puts headings and prompts into the box. Everything you type
afterwards is yours, and you can delete any part of it.

<Callout color="teal" title="Templates come from your organisation">
The list you see was set up by your organisation for your services. If a
template you need is missing, or one is worded badly, that is something your
manager can change — it is not fixed in the software.
</Callout>

## Drafts

An activity can be saved as a **Draft** if you are not finished.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/activities/02-activity-log.png"
  alt="A logged activity marked as a draft alongside the form, with three numbered areas"
  caption="Drafts are visible to you and marked clearly. They are not counted as completed work until you finish them."
/>

1. **The timeline** — everything recorded about this person, newest first. The
   buttons above it narrow the list to one kind, and anything unfinished is
   marked **Draft**.
2. **Log new** — starts another one.
3. **The three tabs** — *Essentials* is what happened, *Notes* is what you
   write, *Follow-up* is what happens next. **Save Draft** at the bottom keeps
   it without finishing; **Complete Activity** files it.

Drafts are useful when you are writing up in the car and want to check
something before you commit. Do not let them pile up — an unfinished note is
not a record.

## If it does not look like this

- **You cannot find the Activities tab** — you may be on the dashboard rather
  than inside a person's record. Open them first.
- **The people list is empty when you try to say who was there** — nobody has
  been added to their support network yet. See [taking on someone
  new](/manual/intake-officer/taking-on-someone-new).
- **There are no templates** — your organisation has not set any up for this
  service.
- **You cannot change something you wrote earlier** — only the person who
  wrote an activity can edit it, and only for 10 days afterwards. After that the
  **Edit** option no longer appears. Nobody else can change it for you, not even
  your supervisor. If it needs correcting, record a new activity saying so rather
  than trying to alter the old one.
