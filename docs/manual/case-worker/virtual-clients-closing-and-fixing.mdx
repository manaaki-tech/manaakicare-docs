# Closing a person on a PO case, and fixing a wrong link

**By the end of this page** you will be able to close one person's involvement
in a PO case, take back a closure request you sent by mistake, and move a person
to the right virtual client if you linked the wrong one.

A PO case can have several people on it. You close each person separately. Closing
one person does not close the PO case or anyone else on it.

<Callout color="teal" title="Where to start">
Both jobs start on the PO case page. Find the person in the **People on this
case** card, then open the menu (the three dots, **⋮**) on their row.
</Callout>

## Closing a person on a PO case

Close a person when the service will no longer record work for them on this
PO case. After you close them, **work will no longer be recorded on this case**.
You cannot add notes for a closed person on that case.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/01-person-row-menu.png"
  alt="The row menu for a person on a PO case, open, with Request closure at the bottom of the list."
  caption="Request closure is in the menu on the person's row."
/>

1. Open the PO case.
2. In **People on this case**, find the person.
3. Open the menu (**⋮**) on their row.
4. Choose **Request closure**.

**Request closure** only appears when the person's status is **Open**. As a
case worker, you also have to be assigned to that person's virtual client.
Until you are, the menu shows only **Add note for this person**. You can assign
yourself: open the virtual client page and press **Manage** on the **Assigned
case workers** card. See [notes, follow-ups and who looks
after a virtual client](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-notes-and-follow-ups#assigned-case-workers).
If you still cannot see it, read [If your screen does not match this page](#if-your-screen-does-not-match-this-page).

### Who can close straight away

The dialog you get depends on the service, not on your job title alone.

<Callout color="amber" title="Close immediately or Send for approval?">
Each service has a setting that lists which positions can close a person
directly. Out of the box, that is supervisors and managers. An administrator can
change the list for a service, and a change can take up to 24 hours to apply.

- If your position is on the list, the dialog offers a **Closure Type** choice:
  **Close immediately** or **Send for approval**.
- If it is not, there is no **Closure Type** choice. Your request goes to
  approval.

So you might see **Close immediately** on one service and not on another. A
colleague with the same title on a different service may see something
different.
</Callout>

Only some people may ask for a closure at all. That is an assigned case worker
for that person, or a manager or supervisor of the service.

### Fill in the closure dialog

The dialog opens with the text *Close this person's involvement in this PO case.
Work will no longer be recorded on this case.*

If you can close straight away and you choose **Close immediately**:

1. Leave **Closure Type** on **Close immediately**.
2. In **End Date**, enter the date the work really ended. It cannot be in the future.
3. In **Closure Reason**, choose the reason from the list. Your organisation sets this list.
4. If you chose **Other**, write what happened in **Please specify**.
5. In **Notes**, add anything the next person should know. This field is optional.
6. Choose **Close Case**.

The person's status changes to **Closed**. A message tells you the PO closed
successfully.

### Sending a closure for approval

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/03-closure-dialog-send-for-approval.png"
  alt="The closure dialog for approval, with an Approver list, a Pending Follow-ups section and a Submit for Approval button."
  caption="The approval version adds an approver and a choice for each pending follow-up."
/>

If you only have the approval route, or you chose **Send for approval**, fill in
**End Date**, **Closure Reason** and **Notes** as above. Then:

1. In **Approver**, choose who should review the request. The list only shows staff allowed to approve closures for this service.
2. If the dialog lists **Pending Follow-ups**, choose **Mark as resolved** or **Cancel (not needed)** for each one.
3. Choose **Submit for Approval**.

A message tells you the closure request was submitted for approval. The person's
status changes to **Closure requested**. They stay on the PO case until
someone approves.

<Callout color="amber" title="If the Approver list is empty">
You will see *Nobody else in this service can approve closures. Ask an admin to
check the service's approval roles.* You cannot send the request without an
approver. Ask your administrator rather than looking for a way round it.
</Callout>

### Messages you might see

| Message | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| End date is required | **End Date** is empty. |
| End date cannot be in the future | Use today's date or earlier. |
| Closure reason is required | Choose a reason from **Closure Reason**. |
| Please select an approver | Choose someone in **Approver** before you submit. |

### Taking back a closure request

If you sent a request by mistake, you can withdraw it while it is still waiting.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/04-withdraw-closure-request.png"
  alt="A person with the status Closure requested, and their row menu open on Withdraw closure request."
  caption="Withdraw closure request only appears while a request is waiting."
/>

1. Open the PO case.
2. Open the menu (**⋮**) on the person's row.
3. Choose **Withdraw closure request**.

A message tells you the closure request was withdrawn. The person goes back to
the status they had before you asked. Only the person who sent the request, or a
manager or supervisor, can withdraw it.

### What happens if an approver rejects it

A rejected request also puts the person back to their previous status. They are
not closed.

<Callout color="amber" title="Do not wait to be told">
We have not confirmed that the system tells you when a request is approved or
rejected. Check the person's status on the PO case page. If it is still
**Closure requested** and you need an answer, ask the approver directly.

When an approver approves, they can change the end date, reason or notes you
sent. What is recorded may not match your request exactly.
</Callout>

### What closing means

<Callout color="terracotta" title="Closing is final">
Once a closure is approved, or you close immediately, there is no button to
reopen that person on that PO case. If the person comes up in a new Police
referral, that is a new PO case and a new link. See
[When the person turns up again](#when-the-person-turns-up-again).
</Callout>

- **Pending follow-ups.** In the approval route you decide what happens to each
  one in the dialog. See
  [notes and follow-ups](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-notes-and-follow-ups).
- **The virtual client.** If this was the last open PO case for a person who has
  never agreed to work with the service, the virtual client's status changes to
  **Closed**.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/05-virtual-client-closed.png"
  alt="A virtual client page showing the status Closed, with its one PO case also closed."
  caption="When the last open PO case closes, a virtual client who never agreed to work with the service becomes Closed."
/>

### People who are already in a care journey

You cannot close a person here once they are in a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.
**Request closure** is not offered for them. Their work closes when the
<Term path="serviceEpisode" /> closes, in the normal way.

When the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> ends, the virtual client becomes **Closed**.

If someone is on a PO case when they agree to work with the service, any
waiting closure request for them is closed automatically. The service records
this as *Closed automatically at consent*.

## When the person turns up again

Someone may be named in a later Police email. When you search for them on the
PO case page, the result row tells you what state they are in. The full list of
result messages is in
[the search results table](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-register-and-add-people#read-the-results).

Two results matter after a closure.

**If they never agreed to work with the service and were closed**, the row reads
*Closed, not consented. You will be asked whether to activate them.*

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/06-activate-question.png"
  alt="The question Activate for this PO case, with Yes, reopen work for this person and No, record it as history only."
  caption="Choosing a closed person who never agreed to work with the service brings up this question."
/>

1. On that row, choose **Choose**.
2. Answer **Activate for this PO case?**
3. Pick **Yes, reopen work for this person** if you will be working with them on this PO case.
4. Or pick **No, record it as history only** if you are only recording that they were named.
5. Choose **Add person to case**.

If you skip the question, you see *Choose whether to activate this person for the PO case.*

**If they agreed to work with the service and that work has finished**, the row
reads *Consented, care journey closed. Start a new virtual client.* You can
only choose **Create new**. The old virtual client is never reopened.

<Callout color="amber" title="What we have not confirmed">
The source for this page does not say exactly what **No, record it as history
only** changes beyond its label. It also does not say whether answering **Yes**
makes the person show up again when intake staff search in New Entry. A closed
virtual client who never agreed to work with the service does drop out of that
search. If you need a person to be findable there, check the result with your
manager.
</Callout>

## Fixing a wrong link

You linked a person to the wrong virtual client, or created a new one when one
already existed. You can move that one person, on that one PO case, to the right
virtual client.

<Callout color="teal" title="One person at a time">
There is no way to merge two virtual clients. Moving a person only moves them
on this PO case. If the same mistake is on three PO cases, you fix it three
times.
</Callout>

### Who can do this

A manager or supervisor can do it. So can a case worker who is assigned to the
virtual client the person is **currently** linked to. If the option is not in
your menu, you are not in one of those groups.

### Move the person

1. Open the PO case.
2. Open the menu (**⋮**) on the person's row.
3. Choose **Link to a different virtual client**.

The **Relink person** dialog opens. It says *Move Police person number … to the
virtual client it belongs to.* A strip at the top shows where the person is
moving from and where they are moving to.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/07-relink-dialog.png"
  alt="The Relink person dialog with the right virtual client chosen, a reason typed, and the Relink button ready."
  caption="Pick the right virtual client, write why, then relink."
/>

1. Search for the virtual client the person belongs to. You can choose a new one if none exists.
2. Choose the right result.
3. In **Reason**, say why the person belongs there. For example, matching names in the Police email.
4. Choose the button at the bottom. It reads **Relink to VC-…** with the number, or **Relink to a new virtual client**.

The **Reason** is required. It is kept in the history of both virtual clients.

When it works, a message says who moved from where, and how many notes and
follow-ups went with them.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closing-and-fixing/08-relink-result.png"
  alt="The PO case page after a relink, with the success message and the person's row showing the new virtual client."
  caption="The message says how many notes and follow-ups moved."
/>

### What moves, and what does not

| Moves with the person | Does not move |
|-----------------------|---------------|
| Police person number | Assigned case workers |
| Role on this case | |
| Status on this case | |
| Address given on this case | |
| This PO case's notes and follow-ups | |

Assigned case workers do not move. Assign them on the new virtual client if they
should still see this person.

We have not confirmed that anyone is told about a relink. If the old virtual
client had other staff on it, tell them yourself.

What happens to the two virtual clients depends on their state:

- **The old virtual client has nothing left on it.** It is removed.
- **The old virtual client never agreed to work with the service and has no
  open PO cases left.** Its status becomes **Closed**.
- **The new virtual client is closed and never agreed to work with the service.**
  It is reopened, as long as the person you moved is not closed.
- **The new virtual client is already in a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.** The
  PO case is attached to it, and the notes are copied onto the
  <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

### When it is refused

Relinking is refused if:

- the person is in a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />,
- any of the person's notes have already been copied onto a
  <Term path="serviceEpisode" />,
- the virtual client you chose is already on this PO case,
- the virtual client you chose is the one they are on now, or
- the virtual client you chose has agreed to work with the service and that work
  has closed.

The source for this page does not give the wording of these refusals. If you are
refused, check the list above for the reason.

### Messages you might see

| Message | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| Choose a person from the search, or create a new one. | You have not chosen a virtual client yet. |
| Enter a reason | **Reason** is empty. |

## If your screen does not match this page

- **There is no Request closure in the menu.** The person may already be
  closed, may already have a request waiting, or may be in a
  <Term path="serviceEpisode" />. You may also not be assigned to their virtual client. Press **Manage** on the
  virtual client page to assign yourself, or ask a manager.
- **There is no Closure Type choice.** That is normal if your position is not on the
  service's list. Your request goes to approval.
- **There is no Withdraw closure request.** It only appears while a request is
  waiting, and only for the person who asked, or a manager or supervisor.
- **There is no Link to a different virtual client.** Only managers, supervisors
  and the case worker assigned to the current virtual client see it.
- **The Approver list is empty.** Ask your administrator to check who can approve
  closures on the service.
- **Relink says no, and nothing here explains why.** Tell your manager what the
  screen said. Do not try to work around it by creating a duplicate.
- **The wording is different from this page.** Say what you see to your manager.
  The screen is right and this page needs fixing.
