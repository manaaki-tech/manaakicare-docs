# Working with someone

**By the end of this page** you will be able to read someone's overview page,
change who is working with them, and fill in what the service needs to know.

<PictureWords />

## The overview page

Open anyone from your list and this is the first thing you see. It is the
single most useful screen in the system — everything else is a detail of it.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/01-overview.png"
  alt="A person's overview page, with their details, assigned staff, compliance panel and recent activity"
  caption="Everything about one person, on one page. Nothing here needs to be filled in straight away."
/>

Reading it, roughly clockwise:

- **The banner at the top** — if the work has not started yet, this is where you
  start it.
- **Reason for entry** — why they came to you, in the words of whoever took the
  enquiry.
- **The row of numbers** — days active, activities logged, staff assigned,
  tasks outstanding.
- **<Term path="serviceEpisode" />** — the reference number, the service, and
  when it started.
- **Staff assigned** — who is walking alongside them.
- **Compliance**, down the right — the paperwork with deadlines. It has [its own
  page](/manual/staying-on-top-of-deadlines).
- **Intake progress** — what is still missing from their intake.
- **Recent activities** — the last few things anyone did.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/03-overview-tour.png"
  alt="The same overview page with each region numbered"
  caption="The same page, numbered. If someone refers to 'the compliance panel' or 'the service data card', this is where they are."
/>

## Changing who works with them

Click **Manage** on the staff card.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/05-manage-staff.png"
  alt="The manage staff dialog listing currently assigned staff"
  caption="One person is marked Primary. That is who the work belongs to — the others are supporting."
  narrow
/>

To add somebody, click **Add Staff Member** and pick them from the list.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/06-add-a-staff-member.png"
  alt="The add staff member dialog with an empty selection list"
  caption="If the person you want is not in the list, they may not be set up for this service. Your administrator can check."
  narrow
/>

<Callout color="teal" title="Adding is not replacing">
Adding a second person does not remove the first. If you are handing work over
rather than sharing it, add the new person, make them **Primary**, and then
remove yourself.
</Callout>

## What the service needs to know

Each service asks its own extra questions. They sit in the **Service data**
card, and they are usually the last thing anyone fills in.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/07-service-fields.png"
  alt="Service-specific fields showing risk levels, next to a feed of recent activity"
  caption="The counter — '0 of 10 collected' — is what a funder eventually asks about. It does not have to be finished today."
/>

Click **Edit** to fill them in. They save as you go.

## Pausing the work

If someone is temporarily out of contact — in hospital, away, or you simply
cannot reach them — you can put the work on hold rather than closing it.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/04-actions-menu.png"
  alt="The actions menu open, showing put on hold and exit options"
  caption="Put on Hold keeps the record open. The other option ends it. They are not the same, and only one is easy to undo."
  narrow
/>

<Callout color="amber" title="On hold is not closed">
Use **Put on Hold** when you expect to pick the work back up. It can be undone —
a **Resume** option brings the work back to active.

Use the option that ends the work — **Exit** or **Close Episode**, depending on
your organisation's wording — only when it has genuinely finished. That one
cannot be undone. See [finishing up](/manual/finishing-up).
</Callout>

## If it does not look like this

- **There is no Manage button** — you may not have permission to change staff
  on this service. Ask a supervisor.
- **The staff list is empty when you try to add someone** — ask your
  administrator whether anyone else is set up on that service.
- **The service data card is not there** — that service has no extra questions.
- **The banner still says the service has not started** — nobody has clicked
  **Start working with...** yet. Until they do, the deadline clock is not
  running.
