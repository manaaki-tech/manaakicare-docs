# Safety alerts

**By the end of this page** you will know how to raise a safety alert, how a
manager decides on one, where alerts show, and who can see them.

Each video is under a minute. Watch the ones that match your job.

<Callout color="amber" title="Only raise an alert if it changes how the work is done">
A safety alert warns the team working with a whānau about a risk to staff.
</Callout>

## 1. What a safety alert is

The three levels, the raise form, and what happens after you raise one.

- **Low** — be aware.
- **Medium** — take the precautions.
- **High** — serious risk, follow every precaution.

<Video id="I_pK1CGvCMI" title="What a safety alert is" />

## 2. Raise an alert

A <Term path="caseWorker" /> raises an alert from the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.
A manager of the service must approve it. Until then, staff on the
<Term path="serviceEpisode" /> see it as awaiting approval.

<Video id="QZmo80LkDdY" title="Raise an alert" />

## 3. Raise an alert as a manager

A manager of the service can raise and approve an alert straight away, or ask
another manager to decide.

<Video id="m9L47OmdeSw" title="Raise an alert as a manager" />

## 4. Approve an alert

Alerts waiting for you are on your dashboard, under **Pending Safety Alert
Confirmations**. Only a manager of that service can approve.

<Video id="lzlyIuEffzs" title="Approve an alert" />

## 5. Approve with changes

A manager can change the level or the precautions before approving.

<Video id="1NdpQFv08so" title="Approve an alert with changes" />

## 6. Decline an alert

Decline with a short reason. The reason is saved. Colleagues do not see a
declined alert.

<Video id="Y4m_LJhSeDc" title="Decline an alert" />

## 7. Who sees what

If you are allocated to the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, you see the full
alert: the level, the precautions, and why it was raised. Other services only
see that an alert exists, and which service holds it.

<Video id="2XOiFNaOpYY" title="Who sees what" />

## 8. Where alerts show

On the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> overview, on the client page, and as a
marker next to the person's name in lists.

<Video id="-0ZtXY4rU-Y" title="Where alerts show" />

## 9. End an alert

When the risk has passed, a manager of the service ends the alert and gives a
reason. Only an active alert can be ended. An alert still waiting for approval
is declined instead.

<Video id="kY5oC5mqddk" title="End an alert" />
