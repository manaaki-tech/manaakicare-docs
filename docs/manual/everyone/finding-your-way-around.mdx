# Finding your way around

**By the end of this page** you will know what the menu down the left does, and
how to get back when you are lost.

<PictureWords />

## The menu down the left

It is always there, and it is how you move between the main parts of the system.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/navigation/left-menu.png"
  alt="The left-hand menu showing the main sections of the system"
  caption="Your menu may have fewer items than this one. What you see depends on your job."
/>

What the items mean:

| Menu item | What is in there |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your starting point — what needs you today |
| **Entries** | New people coming in. Only intake staff, supervisors and managers see it |
| **In-progress Journeys** | Work that is under way |
| **Completed Care** | Work that has finished |

Below a line there are three more items:

| Menu item | What is in there |
|---|---|
| **<Term path="serviceUser" plural />** | The people you work with |
| **Organisations** | Other agencies you deal with. Not everyone sees this |
| **Templates** | Your organisation's forms, consent templates and policy documents |

<Callout color="teal" title="Your menu is not everyone's menu">
The system shows you what your job needs. A <Term path="caseWorker" /> does not
see the same items as an intake officer or a manager, and **Organisations**
only appears for some jobs.

If someone describes a menu item you cannot find, you probably do not have that
part of the job — that is normal, not a fault.
</Callout>

<Callout color="amber" title="Templates here are not note templates">
The **Templates** item in this menu holds organisation-wide documents. The
ready-made wording you drop into a case note is a different thing, and you get
to it from inside the activity form — see [writing up what you
did](/manual/case-worker/writing-up-what-you-did).
</Callout>

## The bar across the top

When you open someone's <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, a bar appears across the top with their name.
The tabs sit in the same bar.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/02-person-header.png"
  alt="The top bar of a person's record, with the name bar marked one and the phone button marked two"
  caption="The name is always up here, so you can tell at a glance whose record you have open."
/>

The two marked parts of that bar:

1. **Their name, age and status** — this stays at the top as you move around
   their record, so you always know whose record you have open.
2. **Their phone number** — click the name bar to open the details. If a number is recorded, it is there, and you can tap it to call.

The tabs are:

- **Overview** — the summary. Start here.
- **Entry Details** — everything from when they came in.
- **Activities** — what has been done with them.
- **Documents** — files that have been uploaded.

You can move between tabs freely. Nothing is lost by switching.

## Getting back

- **To get back to the start** — click **Dashboard** in the left menu.
- **To go back one step** — use your browser's back button. It works normally
  here.
- **To find a person** — open **<Term path="serviceUser" plural />** in the left menu and use the search box at the top of that page.

<Callout color="sage" title="You cannot break it by clicking around">
Opening tabs, looking at records and moving between sections changes nothing.
The system only saves when you click a button that says so — **Save**,
**Continue**, **Submit**.

Have a look around. It is the fastest way to get comfortable.
</Callout>

## If it does not look like this

- **The left menu is missing** — click the small panel icon at the top left to
  bring it back.
- **You cannot find a person** — they may not be assigned to you. You only see
  people you work with. Ask your supervisor.
- **A tab is missing** — not everyone sees every tab. Ask a colleague what they
  see before assuming something is wrong.
