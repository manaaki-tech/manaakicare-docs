# Signing in

**By the end of this page** you will be signed in and looking at your own
dashboard.

## What you need

Your **Microsoft work account** — the same one you use for your work email.

There is no separate Manaaki Central password. You do not need to create an
account, and there is nothing extra to remember.

## Doing it

1. Open your browser and go to your organisation's Manaaki Central address.
2. Click **Sign In with Microsoft**.
3. Enter your work email and password on the Microsoft page that appears.
4. If your phone asks you to approve the sign-in, approve it.

You land on your dashboard. That is your starting point every day.

<Callout color="amber" title="Set up your phone before you need it">
The first time you sign in on a new computer, Microsoft will ask you to confirm
it is really you, using the **Microsoft Authenticator** app on your phone.

Sort that out with your administrator *before* your first day using the system —
not while you have someone waiting in front of you.
</Callout>

## When you finish for the day

Click **Sign out** at the bottom of the left-hand menu.

On a shared computer, sign out and then close the browser. Just closing the
window is not enough — the next person may still be in your session.

## If it will not let you in

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The button says **Initializing...** and nothing happens | Wait a few seconds. If it stays like that, refresh the page. |
| Microsoft accepts you, then Manaaki Central turns you away | Your account has not been linked yet. Ask your administrator. |
| You cannot get to your Authenticator app | Your administrator has to reset it. There is no way to do it yourself. |
| It says your account is locked | Ask your administrator. |

There is more detail, including how sessions expire, on the [full logging in
page](/getting-started/logging-in).
