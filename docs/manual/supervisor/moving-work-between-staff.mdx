# Moving work between staff

**By the end of this page** you will be able to add someone to a piece of work,
hand it over properly, and understand what **Primary** means.

<PictureWords />

## Who this page is for

Changing who is assigned needs permission that a <Term path="caseWorker" /> does
not have. If you are a supervisor or a programme manager you will see a
**Manage** button on the staff card; if you cannot see it, this is not your job
and you should not need this page.

## Opening the list

Open the person's record and click **Manage** on the staff card.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/05-manage-staff.png"
  alt="The manage staff dialog listing currently assigned staff"
  caption="One person is marked Primary. That is who the work belongs to — the others are supporting."
  narrow
/>

**Primary** is the important word. More than one person can be assigned, but
exactly one is Primary, and that is who the work is understood to belong to.

## Adding somebody

Click **Add Staff Member** and pick them from the list.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/06-add-a-staff-member.png"
  alt="The add staff member dialog with an empty selection list"
  caption="If the person you want is not in the list, they may not be set up for this service. Your administrator can check."
  narrow
/>

<Callout color="teal" title="Adding is not replacing">
Adding a second person does not remove the first. Both are now assigned, and
whoever was Primary still is.

If you are handing work over rather than sharing it, do all three steps: add the
new person, make them **Primary**, then remove the previous one. Stopping after
the first step leaves two people each assuming the other has it.
</Callout>

## Handing work over

The order matters, because the system will not leave a piece of work with nobody
on it:

1. **Add** the person taking it on.
2. **Make them Primary.**
3. **Remove** the person handing it over.

Doing it the other way round — removing first — will not work. The last
remaining staff member cannot be removed, and the person marked Primary cannot
be removed directly; make somebody else Primary first.

<Callout color="amber" title="Tell them, as well as the system">
Reassigning changes what appears on someone's dashboard, but it does not tell
them why. A handover still needs the conversation about where the work has got
to.
</Callout>

## If it does not look like this

- **There is no Manage button** — you do not have permission to change staff on
  this service. That is a question for your administrator, not a fault.
- **The person you want is not in the list** — they may not be set up for this
  service. Your administrator can check their positions.
- **You cannot remove somebody** — they are either the only person assigned, or
  they are Primary. Assign someone else, or move Primary first.
