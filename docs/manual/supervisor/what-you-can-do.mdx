# What a supervisor or Service Manager can do

This page is the boundary of your job: what you can see, what you can change, and what stays with the person doing the work. If a button someone describes is missing for you, the answer is usually here.

The two roles do the same things. What differs is **how much you can see** — a supervisor sees their own team's <Term path="activity" plural />, a Service Manager sees everything in the services they work in. Only one action is genuinely restricted to managers, and it is marked below.

## Overview

Supervisors lead teams of <Term path="caseWorker" plural />, manage incoming <Term path="referral" /> requests, and ensure <Term path="serviceUser" plural /> receive timely and quality service delivery. They serve as the bridge between Service Managers and frontline <Term path="caseWorker" plural />.

## Key Responsibilities

### <Term path="referral" plural /> Request Management {#referrals-request-management}
- Reviewing incoming <Term path="referral" /> service requests
- Accepting or rejecting <Term path="referral" plural /> with documented reasons
- Allocating accepted <Term path="referral" plural /> to team members
- Ensuring timely processing of urgent and priority <Term path="referral" plural />

### Team Oversight
- Monitoring <Term path="caseWorker" /> <Term path="activity" /> and engagement
- Resolving due follow-ups from the **Activities Needing Follow-up** table
- Checking the **Staff Workload Distribution** chart for how <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> are spread across the team
- Managing workload distribution across team members

### Service Delivery Monitoring
- Tracking active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> under their supervision
- Monitoring <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that are awaiting commencement
- Ensuring <Term path="referral" plural /> progress through the workflow
- Following up on delayed or stalled <Term path="referral" plural />

### Quality Assurance
- Reviewing <Term path="referral" /> assessments for completeness
- Ensuring documentation standards are maintained
- Providing guidance on complex cases
- Escalating issues to Service Managers when needed

## Permissions

| Action | Supervisor | Service Manager |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|
| View <Term path="referral" /> requests | For your services | For your services |
| Accept <Term path="referral" /> requests | Yes | Yes |
| Reject <Term path="referral" /> requests (with reason) | Yes | Yes |
| Allocate <Term path="referral" plural /> to team members | Yes | Yes |
| View <Term path="caseWorker" plural />' records | Those you supervise | Those in your services |
| View <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> | All in your services | All in your services |
| View <Term path="activity" plural /> | Your team's | All in your services |
| View <Term path="serviceUser" /> information | Yes | Yes |
| Approve programs | **No** | **Yes** |
| Access settings | No (admin only) | No (admin only) |

## Data Visibility

Supervisors can see:
- **Their own records** - personal <Term path="activity" plural /> and assignments
- **Supervised staff records** - all records belonging to <Term path="caseWorker" plural /> they supervise
- **Organisation-level <Term path="referral" plural />** - <Term path="referral" /> requests assigned to their services

This scoped visibility ensures supervisors have full oversight of their team without accessing data outside their responsibility.

## Dashboard Features

The <Term path="supervisorDashboard" /> provides a management-focused view with these sections:

- **Three count tiles** - <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> awaiting commencement, high-priority <Term path="referral" plural />, and active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />, with month-to-date figures beneath them
- **Pending <Term path="referral" /> Requests** - Incoming requests with Accept/Reject actions
- **Pending Exit Approvals** - Requests to close a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, to approve or reject
- **Pending Safety Alert Confirmations** and **Safety Alert Access Requests** - Shown only when something is waiting for you
- **Activities Needing Follow-up** - Follow-ups that are due, which you can resolve in the row
- **Pending Closure Requests** and **Virtual client follow-ups** - At the bottom, for people who work with virtual clients

For detailed dashboard documentation, see [Supervisor / Service Manager Dashboard](/manual/supervisor/dashboard).

## Workflow Overview

### <Term path="referral" /> Request Processing {#referral-request-processing}

```
Referral Dispatched → Service Request Created → Supervisor Reviews
    → Accept (allocate to team member) → Service Delivery
    → Reject (with documented reason)
```

### Supervision Hierarchy

```
Service Manager
  └── Supervisor
        └── Case Worker 1
        └── Case Worker 2
        └── Case Worker 3
```

## Best Practices

### Prioritization
- Process **Emergency**, **Crisis** and **Urgent** <Term path="referral" /> requests immediately
- Check the **Pending Requests** number and the **Pending Referral Requests** table at the start of each day
- Check **Activities Needing Follow-up** daily

### Team Management
- Distribute workload evenly across <Term path="caseWorker" plural />
- Use the **Staff Workload Distribution** chart to see who is carrying the most active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />
- Follow up on overdue items in **Activities Needing Follow-up**

### Communication
- Document reasons when rejecting <Term path="referral" plural />
- Escalate complex cases to Service Managers promptly

### Timeliness
- Aim to process new <Term path="referral" /> requests within 24 hours
- Follow up on <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that are awaiting commencement
- Resolve follow-ups on or before their **Follow-up Date**
