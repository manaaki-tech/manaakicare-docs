# Supervisor / Manager <Term path="dashboard" />

The <Term path="supervisorDashboard" /> is your central hub for managing <Term path="referral" /> requests, approvals and follow-ups. By the end of this page you will know what each part of it shows and what you can do from it. Supervisors and managers share the same dashboard.

## Overview


<Screen
  src="/img/dashboards/supervisor/analytics.png"
  alt="The Manager dashboard with three cards, Awaiting Commencement, High Priority Referrals and Active Care Journeys, and a summary line beneath them"
  caption="Three coloured cards and the summary line beneath them. Select a card to open its list."
/>

From top to bottom, the dashboard shows:

1. **Three cards** - <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> awaiting commencement, high priority <Term path="referral" plural />, and active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />. Select a card to open a list beneath it.
2. **A summary line** - **Pending Requests**, **Activities This Month** and **Referrals This Month**.
3. **Pending Referral Requests** - incoming <Term path="referral" plural /> to accept or reject.
4. **Pending Exit Approvals** - requests to close a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.
5. **Pending Safety Alert Confirmations** and **Safety Alert Access Requests** - shown only when something is waiting for you.
6. **Activities Needing Follow-up** - follow-ups that are due.
7. **Pending Closure Requests** and **Virtual client follow-ups** - at the very bottom, and only for some people. See [below](#pending-closure-requests-and-virtual-client-follow-ups).

The page title is **Team Overview**. If you work in only one service, the title is that service's name. The line under it reads *"Monitor team workload, <Term path="referral" plural /> waiting to be sent, and <Term path="serviceUser" /> outcomes."*

### Overview and Services tabs {#overview-and-services-tabs}

If you work in more than one service, you see two tabs, **Overview** and **Services**. If you work in one service, there are no tabs.

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Overview** | Everything in the list above, across all your services |
| **Services** | Pick one service from **Filter by service:** to see the three cards, the summary line and **Pending Referral Requests** for that service only |

Until you pick a service on the **Services** tab, the page says **Select a service** and asks you to choose one from the dropdown.

<Callout color="teal" title="Where is everything else?">
The **Services** tab does not show **Pending Exit Approvals**, the safety alert tables or **Activities Needing Follow-up**. Look on the **Overview** tab for those.
</Callout>

## Analytics Section

The top of the page has three cards and a summary line. Together they give you an at-a-glance view of your workload.

### The three cards

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Awaiting Commencement** | Number of <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> accepted but not yet started. Underneath: "<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> pending start" |
| **High Priority Referrals** | Number of <Term path="referral" /> requests marked urgent, crisis or emergency. Underneath: "Urgent/crisis/emergency" |
| **Active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />** | Total active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> across your services. Underneath: "Currently in progress" |

The number at the end of each bar is how many active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> they are Primary for. Staff who are Primary for none are left out, and the chart shows up to 10 people. |

Under the cards you will see three numbers:

## Pending <Term path="referral" plural /> Requests {#pending-referrals-requests}


This table lists incoming <Term path="referral" /> requests that need your decision. These are <Term path="referral" plural /> that have been dispatched to a service you supervise and need to be accepted or rejected.

### Table Columns

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Reference** | <Term path="referral" /> reference number (e.g., REF-2026-DEFAULT-00045) |
| **Client Name** | The client's name |
| **Service** | The service being requested |
| **Urgency** | How urgent the request is: Routine, Priority, Urgent, Crisis or Emergency |
| **Status** | Where the request is up to |
| **Date** | The date of the request |
| **Actions** | **Accept** and **Reject** buttons |

Select a row, anywhere except the buttons, to open a preview of the <Term path="referral" />.

### Accepting a <Term path="referral" /> {#accepting-a-referral}

When you click **Accept**, the **Accept Referral Request** dialog appears. It shows the reference, the client and the service requested.

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Assign to Staff (optional)** | Optional | Dropdown, starting at **Select a staff member**. Lists staff for this service with their position and email |
| **Notes (optional)** | Optional | Free text. The box reads "Any notes for the caseworker or team…" |

Click **Accept Request** to finish, or **Cancel** to close the dialog.

After accepting:
1. The <Term path="referral" /> service request status changes to **Accepted**
2. A message appears: "Request accepted", followed by the client and service
3. If you chose a staff member under **Assign to Staff (optional)**, a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created for the client with that person assigned as primary responsible
4. The <Term path="caseWorker" /> will see this <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> on their [Case Worker Dashboard](/manual/case-worker/dashboard) under **My <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />**
5. The <Term path="referral" /> progresses forward in the workflow

<Callout color="teal" title="Choose a staff member when you accept">
Choosing someone under **Assign to Staff (optional)** is what assigns the <Term path="caseWorker" /> straight away. If you leave it empty, the request is accepted with nobody assigned yet.
</Callout>

<Callout color="amber" title="If a care journey could not be created">
If the request is accepted but the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> cannot be created, a warning appears: "Request accepted but <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> creation failed. Please allocate manually." The request itself is still accepted.
</Callout>

### Rejecting a <Term path="referral" /> {#rejecting-a-referral}

When you click **Reject**, the **Reject Referral Request** dialog appears. It requires:

| Field | Required | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| **Rejection Reason** | **Yes** | You must explain why the request cannot be accepted |

The **Reject Request** button stays greyed out until you type a reason. Under the box, the dialog says "A reason is required to reject a request."

After rejecting:
1. The <Term path="referral" /> service request status changes to **Rejected** with the reason recorded
2. The <Term path="referral" /> status reverts to **Ready to Send**
3. The <Term path="referral" /> goes back to the dispatch queue, where a different service can be selected
4. A new service must be selected and a new service request created before the <Term path="referral" /> can proceed again

<Callout color="amber" title="Rejection Reason Required">
You cannot submit a rejection without providing a reason. This ensures transparency and helps the referring team understand the decision. After rejection, the <Term path="referral" /> goes back to the dispatch queue so a different service can be selected and a new request submitted.
</Callout>

### Columns, rows and pages

- Click **Columns** above the table to show or hide columns
- Click a column heading with arrows (**Client Name**, **Service**, **Urgency**, **Status**, **Date**) to sort
- Under the table, **Showing X–Y of Z** tells you which rows you are looking at
- **Rows** sets how many rows appear on a page: 10, 20 or 50
- **Page X of Y** with the arrows moves between pages

### When the table is empty

When there are no pending requests, the table shrinks to its heading and **(0 entries)**: **Pending Referral Requests (0 entries)**. Nothing else appears under it.

## Pending Exit Approvals {#pending-exit-approvals}

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-12-exit-approval/04-pending-exit-approvals.png"
  alt="The Pending Exit Approvals table listing client, service, who submitted the request, the end date, when it was submitted, and Approve and Reject buttons on each row"
/>

On services where closing a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> needs approval, the requests waiting on you collect here.

### Table Columns

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Client** | Who the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is for |
| **Service** | The service being exited |
| **Submitted By** | The staff member asking to close it |
| **End Date** | The end date they've requested |
| **Submitted** | When the request was made: **Today**, or a number of days such as **3d ago** |
| **Actions** | **Approve** and **Reject** |

### What You Can Do

- **Approve** straight from the row, without opening anything
- Select a row to open **Exit Request Review** and read the full request first. It shows the client, service, reference, requested end date, when it was submitted, who submitted it and the assigned approver. Then **Approve** or **Reject** from there
- **View Care Journey** in that dialog opens the full record if you need more context
- **Reject** requires a reason. It's recorded on the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />'s <Term path="activity" /> timeline as an **Exit Rejected** entry — but nobody is notified, and nothing points the requester to it, so message them as well

Clicking **Reject** on a row opens the same dialog with the rejection form already showing. Type the reason, then click **Confirm Rejection**.

Approving closes the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> using the details in the request. Rejecting leaves it open so the work can be finished and resubmitted.

### When nothing is waiting

The table shrinks to its heading and **(0 entries)**: **Pending Exit Approvals (0 entries)**.

See [Manager approval for <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> exits](/releases/2026-09-12-exit-approval) for the full workflow, including what the person requesting the exit sees.

## Pending Safety Alert Confirmations {#pending-safety-alert-confirmations}

This table lists safety alerts waiting for you to confirm. It only appears when there is at least one. If there is nothing to confirm, you will not see it at all.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Client** | The client the alert is about |
| **Alert Level** | LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH |
| **Created By** | Who raised the alert |
| **Date Raised** | When it was raised |
| **Actions** | **View** and **Review** |

- **Review**, or selecting the row, opens the alert so you can review it
- **View** opens the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> the alert came from

On a narrow screen, **Created By** and **Date Raised** are hidden.

## Safety Alert Access Requests {#safety-alert-access-requests}

This table lists requests from other services to see a client's safety alerts. Like the table above, it only appears when something is waiting for you.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Client** | The client the request is about |
| **Requesting Service** | The service asking for access |
| **Alerts** | How many alerts the request covers |
| **Requested By** | Who asked |
| **Highest Level** | The highest alert level among them: LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH |
| **Date Requested** | The earliest request date |
| **Actions** | **View** and **Review** |

Requests from the same service about the same client are combined into one row. **Review**, or selecting the row, opens it for your decision. **View** opens the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

## Activities Needing Follow-up {#activities-needing-follow-up}

<Screen
  src="/img/dashboards/supervisor/activities-needing-follow-up.png"
  alt="The Activities Needing Follow-up table with Reference, Client, Service, Follow-up Date and Priority columns, and one row expanded to resolve the follow-up"
  caption="The tick button opens the resolve panel inside the row."
/>

This table lists follow-ups that are due. It includes your own follow-ups and those of the staff you supervise. Use it to resolve a follow-up without leaving the dashboard.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Reference** | The reference number |
| **Client** | The client's name |
| **Service** | The service |
| **Follow-up Date** | When the follow-up is due |
| **Priority** | The priority badge |

Each row also has two controls on the right:

- The **tick** button (hover text **Resolve follow-up**) opens a panel inside the row, where you record what happened
- The **three dots** menu (hover text **More options**) offers **Log activity**, which opens the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />'s <Term path="activity" plural /> page, and **No longer needed**, which asks you to confirm first

The steps for resolving a follow-up are in [Resolving a follow-up](/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution).

When nothing is due, the table is hidden and you see only the heading with **(0 entries)**: **Activities Needing Follow-up (0 entries)**. If you work in more than one service, this table is on the **Overview** tab only.

## Pending Closure Requests and Virtual client follow-ups {#pending-closure-requests-and-virtual-client-follow-ups}

These two tables sit at the very bottom of the dashboard. They only appear for some people.

### Pending Closure Requests

This table appears only if your position is allowed to approve closures directly in a service that uses virtual clients.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **PO Case** | The case reference, with the person number underneath |
| **Virtual Client** | The virtual client's reference |
| **Requested By** | Who asked, and when |
| **Reason** | The closure reason, with any other reason they wrote |
| **End Date** | The requested end date |
| **Status** | **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** or **Withdrawn** |

Rows you can approve have a **Review** button. Selecting a row opens the same review. Rows you cannot approve do nothing when selected. When nothing is waiting, the table shows **Pending Closure Requests (0 entries)**.

### Virtual client follow-ups

This table appears only if one of your services uses virtual clients. It lists your own pending follow-ups on virtual client notes.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Note** | The title of the note |
| **Due Date** | The follow-up date, with **(overdue)** beside it when it has passed |
| **Case / Client** | A link to the case, or to the virtual client for a general note |

Each row has **Resolve** and **Cancel** buttons. When nothing is due, the table shows **Virtual client follow-ups (0 entries)**. For more, see [Virtual clients](/releases/2026-11-09-virtual-clients).

## Common Tasks

### Searching

The main tables have no search box. Two places do:

| Where | Search Placeholder |
|-------|--------------------|
| **Awaiting Commencement** panel | "Search client name..." |
| **High Priority Referrals** panel | "Search name or reference..." |

Results filter as you type. Click **Clear** beside the box to start again.

### Pagination

When there are many records, pagination controls appear at the bottom of each table:

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Showing X–Y of Z** | Indicates which records are currently displayed |
| **Rows** | How many rows per page: 10, 20 or 50 |
| **Page X of Y** | Current page number and total pages |
| **< >** arrows | Navigate to previous/next page |

### Refreshing Data

The dashboard has no refresh button. Reload the page in your browser to bring in the latest numbers.

## <Term path="referral" /> Request Workflow {#referral-request-workflow}

### Status Flow for Service Requests

```
Pending → Accepted → Case Worker Assigned → Service Delivery
         → Rejected (with reason)
```

### What Happens on Accept

1. Service request status changes to **Accepted**
2. If you choose a staff member under **Assign to Staff (optional)**:
   - A <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created for the client under the requested service
   - The <Term path="caseWorker" /> is **assigned to the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />** as primary responsible
   - The <Term path="caseWorker" /> sees this <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> on their dashboard
3. The <Term path="referral" /> progresses to active service delivery

### What Happens on Reject

1. Service request status changes to **Rejected** with the reason recorded
2. The <Term path="referral" /> status reverts to **Ready to Send**
3. The <Term path="referral" /> goes back to the dispatch queue, where a new service can be selected
4. A new service request must be created before the <Term path="referral" /> can be sent to a supervisor again

## Data Visibility

Supervisors and managers have different data scopes:

| Role | Data Scope |
|------|------------|
| **Supervisor** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> in their services, and the <Term path="activity" plural /> of their own team |
| **manager** | All records in the services they work in |
| **Admin** | All records within their organisation |

## Tips for Efficient Work

### Daily Routine

1. **Start of Day**
   - Check the **Pending Requests** number under the cards for new <Term path="referral" /> requests
   - Accept or reject requests in **Pending Referral Requests**
   - Check **Pending Exit Approvals** and, if shown, the safety alert tables

2. **Throughout the Day**
   - Choose a staff member when you accept, so the <Term path="caseWorker" /> is assigned straight away
   - Open **Awaiting Commencement** to see <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that have not started
   - Resolve what is due in **Activities Needing Follow-up**

3. **End of Day**
   - Review any remaining pending requests
   - Open **Staff Workload Distribution** to check how work is spread across the team
   - Look at **Activities Needing Follow-up** for anything still due

### Priority Management

| Priority | Expected Response | Action |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| **Emergency** or **Crisis** | Immediate | Accept and allocate immediately |
| **Urgent** | Same day | Process as soon as possible |
| **Priority** | Within 24 hours | Process promptly |
| **Routine** | Within 48 hours | Process in order received |

### Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Esc` | Close dialogs |

## Troubleshooting

### Dashboard Not Loading

1. Check your internet connection
2. Reload the page in your browser
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Clear your browser cache

### No Pending Requests Showing

- This is normal if all requests have been processed
- The heading **Pending Referral Requests (0 entries)** confirms there are no pending items
- New requests will appear automatically when submitted
- On the **Services** tab, check that you have picked the right service

### I Cannot See a Table

- **Pending Safety Alert Confirmations** and **Safety Alert Access Requests** only appear when something is waiting for you
- **Pending Closure Requests** and **Virtual client follow-ups** only appear for some people, as described above
- On the **Services** tab, only the cards, the summary line and **Pending Referral Requests** are shown, plus the two tables at the bottom if they apply to you

### Data Not Updating

1. Reload the page in your browser
2. Wait a few moments and try again
3. Check the service filter on the **Services** tab
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### Your screen looks different

If your dashboard does not match this page, tell your administrator which table or button is different. Do not guess what it does.
