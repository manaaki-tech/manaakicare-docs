# Virtual clients: closures, accepting a referral and consent

**By the end of this page** you will be able to close a person's involvement in a
PO case, review closure requests from your team, and know exactly what changes
when you accept a <Term path="referral" /> for a person who started as a virtual
client.

A virtual client is a placeholder for someone named in a Police email. It holds
only a date of birth, a gender and an address. It never holds a name. This page
is for supervisors and managers. The linking step belongs to the intake
officer, and the day-to-day fixes belong to the <Term path="caseWorker" />.

- [Linking a virtual client](/manual/intake-officer/linking-a-virtual-client)
  covers the intake officer's side.
- [Closing and fixing](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-closing-and-fixing)
  covers what your team can do themselves.
- The [overview](/virtual-clients/overview) explains the whole feature.

<Callout color="amber" title="Closing a person on a PO case is final">
Approval cannot be undone, and a closed person cannot be reopened. If the same
person is in a new Police email later, that is a new PO link. Check the end
date and reason before you click.
</Callout>

## What the statuses mean

A virtual client and each person on a PO case show one of these statuses.

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Open** | Work is being recorded. The person has not agreed to work with the service yet. |
| **Closure requested** | Someone has asked to close them. A manager has not decided yet. |
| **In care journey** | The person agreed, the <Term path="referral" /> was accepted, and a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is running. |
| **Closed** | Finished. For a person on a PO case this is final. |

A virtual client also shows a badge. **Pre-consent** means the person has not
agreed yet. **Consented** means the referral was accepted.

The list of virtual clients has a status filter. It offers **All statuses**,
**Open**, **In Care Journey** and **Closed**. It has no **Closure requested**
option, so use the dashboard card below to find requests.

## Closing someone straight away

If your position is allowed to close directly, you can close a person's
involvement in a PO case without asking anyone. Usually that means
supervisors and managers.

1. Open the PO case from the **Virtual Clients** menu.
2. In the **People on this case** table, find the person.
3. Select the three-dot menu on their row.
4. Choose **Request closure**.
5. Under **Closure Type**, choose **Close immediately**.
6. Enter the **End Date**. It cannot be in the future.
7. Choose a **Closure Reason**.
8. If the reason is Other, fill in **Please specify**.
9. Add **Notes** if they help.
10. Choose **Close Case**.

The dialog may also list pending follow-ups for that person. If it does, choose
**Mark as resolved** or **Cancel (not needed)** for each one.

A message confirms the PO case is closed. The person's status changes to
**Closed**.

Three things can stop a closure:

- The person is already **Closed**.
- The person already has a pending closure request.
- The person is in a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />. They close when the care
  journey closes, not here.

If the person was the last open PO case for a virtual client who never agreed to
work with the service, the virtual client becomes **Closed** too.

<Callout color="teal" title="If you do not see Closure Type">
If the **Closure Type** choice is missing, your position is not on the service's
direct-close list. Your request goes to a manager for approval instead. That is
normal, not a fault.
</Callout>

## Reviewing closure requests

When a <Term path="caseWorker" /> asks to close someone, the request waits on the
dashboard of every approver.

1. Open your dashboard.
2. Find the **Pending Closure Requests** card.
3. Find the row you want. The columns are **PO Case**, **Virtual Client**,
   **Requested By**, **Reason**, **End Date** and **Status**.
4. Choose **Review** on that row.

### Approving

1. In the **Review Closure Request** dialog, choose **Approve**.
2. Read the **Approve Closure** panel. It says you can optionally override any
   of the closure details.
3. If you want to change something, edit **Override End Date**,
   **Override Closure Reason** or **Override Notes**.
4. Choose **Approve & Close**.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closures-and-consent/04-approve-with-overrides.png"
  alt="The Approve Closure panel with Override End Date, Override Closure Reason and Override Notes fields and an Approve & Close button."
  caption="Overrides are optional. Leave them empty to approve exactly what was asked."
/>

A message says **Closure request approved. PO123456 is now closed.** (with your
own PO number). The message names the PO case, but only this person is closed.
Anyone else on the case stays open, even though the message says the PO case is
closed. The person's status changes to **Closed**. This is final.

### Rejecting

1. In the **Review Closure Request** dialog, choose **Reject**.
2. Enter a **Rejection Reason**. It is required.
3. Choose **Reject Request**.

If you leave the reason empty you will see **Please provide a rejection reason**.
After you reject, a message says **Closure request rejected**. The person goes
back to the status they had before the request.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/virtual-clients/closures-and-consent/05-reject-with-reason.png"
  alt="The reject panel asking why the closure request is being rejected, with a reason typed in and a Reject Request button."
  caption="A reason is required. Write something the person can act on."
/>

<Callout color="amber" title="Tell them yourself">
The system does not tell anyone when you approve or reject a request. Tell the
person who asked, in person or by message.
</Callout>

### Who can act on a request

Any user who sees the **Pending Closure Requests** card can approve or reject
it. It does not have to be the approver the requester picked. If two managers
open the same request, tell each other who is dealing with it.

You can also withdraw a request. Select the three-dot menu on the person's row,
then **Withdraw closure request**. The person who asked can do this, and so can
a manager or supervisor. The person goes back to their earlier status.

## Who appears on the approver list

The card and the **Approver** choice depend on which positions your service
allows to close directly. Usually those are supervisor and manager. If the
list needs to change, ask your administrator.

If you cannot see the **Pending Closure Requests** card, your position is
probably not on that list.

If a team member sees **Nobody else in this service can approve closures. Ask an
admin to check the service's approval roles.**, nobody is available to approve
for them.

## Accepting a referral is consent

If the intake officer chooses **Send for approval**, nothing changes on the
virtual client when they submit the <Term path="referral" />. The change happens
when you **accept** it.

Most services allocate directly. The intake officer chooses **Accept & assign
Case Worker** when they submit, because a manager has already approved the paper
form. The referral is accepted at that moment, and the same changes happen. You
may never see it as a request to accept.

When you accept, this is exactly what happens:

- The virtual client's badge changes to **Consented**.
- Its status changes to **In care journey**.
- **Consented at** is filled in with the date.
- A <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created.
- The virtual client's notes are copied onto the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, in
  date order. Each note keeps its PO number.
- Its follow-ups are copied across too. Each keeps its status.
- Any pending closure request for that person is closed automatically. It is
  marked **Closed automatically at consent**.

Two things are not copied:

- **Assigned case workers do not move across.** Assign staff to the
  <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> in the normal way.
- **PO cases stay on the virtual client.** They show as links, not copies.

After consent you can no longer edit date of birth or gender on the virtual
client, and the address is read-only. If a detail is wrong, update it on the
person's record instead.

On the virtual client page, the **Care journey** reference (for example
SEP-2026-ORG-00009) is a link. Click it and a side panel opens. It shows the care
journey card, the case workers on it and the recent activities. The virtual
client's notes show there as activities. Press **Open full care journey** in the
panel to go to the care journey itself.

### If you reject the referral

The virtual client goes back to **Open**. The intake officer can link it to a
different <Term path="referral" />.

### If it still says Pre-consent

If a virtual client still shows **Pre-consent** after you accepted the
<Term path="referral" />, tell your administrator. There is no button to try
again.

## When the care journey ends

When the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is exited in the normal way, the virtual
client becomes **Closed**.

If that person is named in a later Police email, searching shows **Consented,
care journey closed. Start a new virtual client.** The intake officer creates a
new virtual client. The old one is never reopened. Both can point to the same
person's record.

## Other manager tasks

### Delete a PO case with no one on it

1. Open the PO case.
2. Open the case actions menu.
3. Choose **Delete case**.
4. Confirm in the **Delete PO case** dialog.

A message says **PO case deleted successfully**.

If **Delete case** is greyed out, the case still has people on it. The page
says **Only a case with no one on it can be deleted.**

### Delete a note

Only managers and supervisors can delete a note. Staff can edit their own.

1. Open the **Notes** section.
2. Select the note in the list.
3. In the reading pane, choose **Delete**.

### Closure reasons

The list of closure reasons belongs to your organisation, not to a service.
You cannot change it yourself. If a reason is missing or wrong, ask your
administrator.

## Known limits

- **No merge.** If the same person ended up as two virtual clients, you cannot
  merge them. A relink moves one person on one PO case at a time.
- **No notifications.** Nobody is told when staff are assigned, a person is
  relinked, or a closure is approved or rejected.
- **Closing does not chain.** Closing a consented person does not prompt you to
  close a linked pre-consent person.
- **Closed virtual clients drop out of New Entry search.** This applies to
  those that never agreed to work with the service.
- **Joint notes can show as two rows.** One note about two people may appear
  twice in some lists.
- **No Closure requested filter.** Use the dashboard card.

## If your screen does not match this page

- **No Virtual Clients menu.** Not every service uses virtual clients, and
  yours may not. Ask your administrator.
- **No Pending Closure Requests card.** Your position may not be on the
  service's approver list. Ask your administrator. A change can take up to 24
  hours to apply.
- **No Closure Type choice.** Your position is not allowed to close directly.
  Your request goes for approval.
- **Delete case is greyed out.** The case still has people on it.
- **Still Pre-consent after acceptance.** Tell your administrator. There is no
  retry button.
- **Anything else.** Take a photo of the screen and send it to your
  administrator. Do not guess.

Also see [Manager approval for exits](/releases/2026-09-12-exit-approval). It
works the same way for <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> exits.
