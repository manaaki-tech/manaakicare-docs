# Your day at a glance

**By the end of this page** you will be able to open your dashboard and know
what needs you first.

<PictureWords />

## Your dashboard

This is what you land on when you sign in.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/dashboard/my-caseload.png"
  alt="The case worker dashboard, with count tiles across the top and a list of assigned work below"
  caption="The tiles across the top are counts. The list underneath is the actual work."
/>

### The tiles across the top

Each one is a count of something that might need you. Click a tile to see what
is behind it.

| Tile | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Active Cases** | People you are working with right now |
| **Urgent / Crisis** | Cases flagged as urgent — look at these first |
| **Follow-ups Due** | Things you said you would come back to |
| **Total <Term path="referral" plural />** | Everything assigned to you, open or not |

### Ready to start

If someone has been assigned to you but the work has not begun, they appear
here with a **Start working with...** button.

<Callout color="amber" title="Starting the work sets the deadlines">
Clicking **Start working with...** does more than tidy your list. It sets the
date that all their paperwork deadlines are counted from.

If you have already been working with them for a fortnight, say so — do not let
today's date become the official start.
</Callout>

### Your list

Underneath is everyone assigned to you, with their reference, their service,
their status and when they started. Click **View** on any row to open them.

## Where to start each day

A reasonable order:

1. **Urgent / Crisis** — anything flagged urgent.
2. **Follow-ups Due** — you told someone you would come back to them.
3. **Ready to start** — work that has been waiting on you to begin.
4. **Everything else** — your ordinary visits and write-ups.

## If you are an intake officer

Your dashboard is a different shape, because your job is different — it is
about what is arriving rather than who you are supporting.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/dashboard/intake-dashboard.png"
  alt="The intake dashboard, showing new entries grouped by status"
  caption="New enquiries arrive here. The tabs across the middle sort them by what stage they have reached."
/>

There is a fuller breakdown on the [intake officer
dashboard](/dashboards/intake-officer) page.

## If it does not look like this

- **Your tiles are all zero** — nothing is assigned to you yet. That is a
  question for whoever allocates work.
- **You see a different dashboard entirely** — dashboards differ by job. See
  [understanding your dashboard](/getting-started/understanding-your-dashboard).
- **A number looks wrong** — refresh the page. The counts are calculated when
  the page loads.
