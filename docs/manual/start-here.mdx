# Start here

This manual is for the people doing the mahi — taking enquiries, visiting
whānau, and writing up what happened. You do not need to read it in order.
Find the thing you are trying to do, and follow the pictures.

## What this system is for

Manaaki Central keeps one record of everyone your organisation is supporting.

When someone new comes to you, you enter them once. From then on, the system
holds who they are, who else is involved in their life, what service they are
getting, what you have done with them, and what still needs doing by when.

The point is that nothing lives only in your head or your notebook. If you are
away, someone else can pick it up. When funders ask what you did, the answer is
already recorded.

## The shape of the work

Almost everything follows the same path:

1. **Someone is referred to you** — by another agency, by their whānau, or they
   walk in.
2. **You decide what happens next** — accept them into a service, or send it to
   a manager to look at first.
3. **Work starts** — a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> opens, and someone is
   assigned to walk alongside them.
4. **You record what you do** — visits, phone calls, and notes.
5. **Deadlines are tracked** — consent forms, assessments, and paperwork, with
   dates.
6. **The work closes** — you write it up and finish.

Each of those steps has its own page in this manual.

## If you get stuck

Every page ends with what to do if the screen does not look like the picture.
There is also a page on [when something looks
wrong](/manual/when-something-looks-wrong) that collects the common ones.

One thing worth knowing early: when you are taking on someone new, the
**<Term path="referral" />** is not saved until you finish the first step and
click **Continue**. After that it is safe to leave and come back. Before that,
closing the page loses what you typed.

The person's own record is different — if you add somebody new part-way
through, they are created as soon as you submit that little form, and they
stay even if you abandon the rest.
