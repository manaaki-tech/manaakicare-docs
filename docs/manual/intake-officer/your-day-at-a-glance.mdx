# Your day at a glance

**By the end of this page** you will be able to open your dashboard and know
what needs you first.

<PictureWords />

## Your dashboard

This is what you land on when you sign in. It is about what is *arriving*,
rather than who you are supporting — that is the difference between your
dashboard and a <Term path="caseWorker" />'s.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/dashboard/intake-dashboard.png"
  alt="The intake dashboard, showing new entries grouped by status"
  caption="New enquiries arrive here. The tabs across the middle sort them by what stage they have reached."

## The tabs across the middle

Each tab is a stage. An enquiry moves left to right as it is dealt with, so the
leftmost tab is where new work appears and the rightmost is work that is nearly
out of your hands.

The count on each tab is how many are sitting at that stage. A number that keeps
growing on one tab is the useful signal — it means work is arriving faster than
it is moving on.

<Callout color="teal" title="The tabs are not a to-do list in order">
You do not have to clear one tab before looking at another. Urgency beats
stage — a crisis enquiry that arrived this morning matters more than something
routine that has been waiting a week.
</Callout>

## Opening one

Click any row and a preview opens over the page — who it is for, the reason,
the consent that is on file, and the urgency. It is **read-only**. Nothing on
your dashboard can be edited from the dashboard itself.

To change anything, open the person's record properly. That is where the tabs
down the left live:

| Section | What you fill in there |
|---|---|
| **Intake Info** | Everything from step 1, editable after the fact |
| **Whānau Background** | Ethnicity, iwi, language, beliefs |
| **Support Network** | Whānau and professionals involved in their life |
| **Consent** | What they agreed to, and the signed form |

A number beside one of those means that many things are still outstanding.
[Taking on someone new](/manual/intake-officer/taking-on-someone-new#filling-in-the-rest)
walks through each of them.

<Callout color="amber" title="Follow-ups do not show on this dashboard">
If you are waiting on something — a consent form, a callback — it will not
appear here as a reminder. Follow-up dates belong to <Term path="activity" plural />,
and <Term path="activity" plural /> are shown on the <Term path="caseWorker" />
dashboard, not this one.

Your equivalent is the **Age** column. An entry sitting at the same stage for
several days is the signal that something has stalled, so sort by it.
</Callout>

## Writing something down

Recording a phone call or a note against somebody is not done from here either.
Open their record, go to the **Activities** tab, and click **Log new** — the
same form a <Term path="caseWorker" /> uses, described in [writing up what you
did](/manual/case-worker/writing-up-what-you-did).

It is worth doing for intake work too. If you rang three times before anybody
answered, that belongs on the record rather than in your head — the person who
picks the work up next has no other way of knowing.

## Where to start each day

A reasonable order:

1. **Anything marked urgent or crisis**, wherever it is sitting.
2. **New enquiries** — get them recorded while the detail is fresh.
3. **Anything waiting on you** — entries you sent for approval that have come
   back, or that have been sitting without a decision.
4. **Everything else.**

## If it does not look like this

- **Your tabs are all empty** — nothing has been submitted yet, or nothing is
  assigned to the services you cover.
- **You see a list of people rather than enquiries** — you are on the
  <Term path="caseWorker" /> dashboard. See [your day at a
  glance](/manual/case-worker/your-day-at-a-glance) for that one.
- **A number looks wrong** — refresh the page. The counts are calculated when
  the page loads.
