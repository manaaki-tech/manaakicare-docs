# Intake Officer <Term path="dashboard" />

The Intake Officer <Term path="dashboard" /> is your central hub for managing the <Term path="referral" /> intake process. It shows <Term path="referral" plural /> at each stage, from new submissions until they are sent to a team.

## Overview

<Screen
  src="/img/dashboards/intake-officer/overview.png"
  alt="The Intake Dashboard with three cards: Entry Processing, Awaiting Service and High Priority"
  caption="Three coloured cards. Click a card to open its list underneath."
/>


The Intake <Term path="dashboard" /> displays:
- **Three cards** — **Entry Processing**, **Awaiting Service** and **High Priority** — each showing a count
- **Four tabs** — **Intake**, **In Review**, **Service Review** and **With Kaiāwhina** — each with a count badge
- A **search box** and filters for priority and <Term path="referral" /> date
- **Click any row** to open a summary of that <Term path="referral" />
- An **Activities Needing Follow-up** table below the tabs
- For some users, extra cards at the bottom (see [Cards you may see below the table](#cards-you-may-see-below-the-table))

The **New Entry** button is not on the dashboard itself. It sits in the top bar. See [Creating a New Referral](#creating-a-new-referral).

## Tab Navigation

<div style={{textAlign: 'center'}}>
  <img src="/img/dashboards/intake-officer/tabs.png" alt="Tab Navigation" />
</div>

The dashboard uses tabs to organise <Term path="referral" plural /> by their current stage. Each tab shows a count badge with the number of <Term path="referral" plural /> in that stage. **Intake** is the tab that opens first.

| Tab | Description | Badge |
|-----|-------------|-------|
| **Intake** | New <Term path="referral" plural /> requiring initial processing | Shows count of <Term path="referral" plural /> in intake |
| **In Review** | <Term path="referral" plural /> with consent recorded, waiting for you to submit them or send them to a team | Shows count in review |
| **Service Review** | <Term path="referral" plural /> already sent to a team, waiting for the team to respond | Shows count pending service review |
| **With Kaiāwhina** | <Term path="referral" plural /> a team has accepted, up to and including service in progress | Shows count in this tab |

<Callout color="teal" title="That last tab always says Kaiāwhina">
Some labels on screen follow your organisation's own words. This one does not. It reads **With Kaiāwhina** for everyone, even if your organisation calls the role something else.
</Callout>

Click any tab to switch the table to show <Term path="referral" plural /> at that stage. Switching tabs clears the search box. Completed and closed <Term path="referral" plural /> are not on any tab.

If a tab has nothing to show, the table is replaced by the words **(0 entries)**.

## Filters

Use the inline filters to narrow down your view:

| Filter | Options | Use Case |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Search** | Free text. The box says **Search by name or reference...** | Find specific <Term path="referral" plural /> quickly |
| **Priority** | All Priorities, Routine, Priority, Urgent, Crisis, Emergency | Focus on high-priority <Term path="referral" plural /> |
| **All dates** | A start and end date, matched against the <Term path="referral" /> date | View <Term path="referral" plural /> from specific time periods |

A **Clear** button appears beside the filters once you choose a priority or a date range. It resets both. It does not empty the search box.

#### How to Use Filters

1. Type a name or reference number in the **search bar**
2. Click the **Priority** dropdown to select a priority level
3. Click the **All dates** field to open the date picker and select a date range
4. The table updates automatically to show filtered results

The **Age** badge changes colour: green under 3 days, amber from 3 to 7 days, and red over 7 days. Hover over it to see the number of days.

### Opening a referral

Click anywhere on a row. A window opens with the person's name at the top and the reference number under it, followed by a summary of the <Term path="referral" />.

At the bottom of the window:

| Button | When you see it | What it does |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Close** | Always | Closes the window |
| **Edit in Wizard** | Status is Draft, Pending Consent, Pending Review or Ready to Send | Opens the intake form with this <Term path="referral" /> loaded, so you can carry on |
| **View Details** | Any other status | Opens the <Term path="referral" />'s full page |

## Intake Tab

This is the tab that opens first. It shows all <Term path="referral" plural /> that are new or in the initial intake processing stage.

### What You'll See

<Term path="referral" plural /> with the following statuses:
- **Draft** — saved part-way through step 1 of the intake form
- **Pending Consent** — awaiting client consent before proceeding

### How to Process New <Term path="referral" plural /> {#how-to-process-new-referrals}

1. Review the list - check priority levels and age
2. Process Emergency and Crisis items first
3. Click a row to open a summary of the <Term path="referral" />, then press **Edit in Wizard** to carry on with it
4. Finish the consent step
5. On the last step, press **Submit Entry**

<Callout color="teal" title="Reference Numbers">
Each <Term path="referral" /> has a unique reference number (e.g., REF-2026-DEFAULT-00047) that you can use to track and search for <Term path="referral" plural />.
</Callout>

---

## In Review Tab

This tab shows <Term path="referral" plural /> that are still with you. Consent is recorded, but they have not been sent to a team yet.

### What You'll See

<Term path="referral" plural /> with the following statuses:
- **Pending Review** — consent is recorded, but the <Term path="referral" /> has not been submitted yet
- **Ready to Send** - <Term path="referral" plural /> ready for you to send to a team. A <Term path="referral" /> that a team rejects also comes back here.

If you choose **Send for approval**, it moves to the **Service Review** tab while the supervisor or manager decides.

### How to Dispatch a <Term path="referral" /> {#how-to-dispatch-a-referral}

Look for **Ready to Send** in the **Status** column of this tab.

1. Click the row of a <Term path="referral" /> to open its summary
2. Press **Edit in Wizard**. The form opens on its last step.
3. Check the summary, then press **Send to Team**
4. Choose **Send for approval**, **Accept entry** or **Accept & assign <Term path="caseWorker" />**, and pick the person if asked
5. Press the button at the bottom. It names where the <Term path="referral" /> is going.

<Callout color="amber" title="Review Delays">
Use the **Age** column to monitor how long <Term path="referral" plural /> have been pending. If an item sits at **Pending Review** for a long time, it has not been submitted yet. Open it and finish the last step.
</Callout>

## Service Review Tab

This tab displays <Term path="referral" plural /> that have been sent to a team and are waiting for the team to respond.

### What You'll See

<Term path="referral" plural /> with status:
- **Service Review** — sent to a team, which has not yet accepted or rejected it

<Term path="referral" plural /> that are ready for you to send are on the **In Review** tab, not here.

---

## With Kaiāwhina Tab

This tab shows <Term path="referral" plural /> that a team has accepted, up to and including service in progress.

### What You'll See

<Term path="referral" plural /> with the following statuses:
- **Pending Allocation** — accepted by a service, awaiting <Term path="caseWorker" /> allocation
- **Awaiting Service** — allocated to a <Term path="caseWorker" />, service has not started yet
- **Service In Progress** — service delivery is underway

Completed and closed <Term path="referral" plural /> do not appear on this tab.

### Monitoring Post-Dispatch <Term path="referral" plural /> {#monitoring-post-dispatch-referrals}

- Track <Term path="referral" plural /> that are awaiting service start using the **Age** column
- Use the **Priority** filter to focus on urgent ones
- Follow up on delayed service initiations

## Activities Needing Follow-up

This table sits below the tabs. It lists follow-ups that are still pending.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Reference** | The reference number |
| **Client** | The client's name |
| **Service** | The service |
| **Follow-up Date** | **Overdue —** and the date, **Due today**, or the date |
| **Priority** | A priority badge |

The last column has no heading. It holds a tick button and a three-dot menu.

### How to Resolve a Follow-up

1. Find the row in the table
2. Press the tick button at the end of the row. Its hover text is **Resolve follow-up**.
3. Check the title in **Completed activity**. It starts with the word Completed.
4. Leave **Completed** selected, or press **Didn't happen**
5. If you chose **Didn't happen**, press a reason under **Why didn't it happen?**: **No-show**, **Unavailable**, **Not fit**, **Rescheduled**, **Client cancelled** or **Staff cancelled**
6. Tick **Needs another follow-up** if one is needed, then choose its date
7. Press **Save & resolve**

The panel closes and the row turns pale green. The panel says it creates an activity on the client's care journey and closes the follow-up. It also says closing can't be undone. If you chose **Didn't happen**, **Save & resolve** stays greyed out until you pick a reason. Press **Cancel** to close the panel without saving.

Press the three-dot menu (hover text **More options**) for two other choices:

- **Log activity** — opens the activity form for that care journey
- **No longer needed** — asks **Cancel Follow-up?**. Press **Yes, Cancel Follow-up** to remove it from your list, or **Keep Follow-up** to leave it.

The table shows **Showing X–Y of Z**, a **Rows** choice of 10, 20 or 50, and **Page X of Y** arrows. For the full steps, see [Resolving a follow-up](/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution).

When no follow-up is pending, the table is hidden. You see only the heading, **Activities Needing Follow-up (0 entries)**.

## Cards you may see below the table

These cards appear only for some users.

### Virtual client follow-ups

You see this card only if you are assigned to a service that uses virtual clients. It lists your own pending follow-ups.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Note** | The title of the note the follow-up came from |
| **Due Date** | The date, with **(overdue)** in red if it has passed |
| **Case / Client** | A link to the case or the virtual client |

Each row has **Resolve** and **Cancel** buttons. Both ask for a **Closing note**. A closed row stays on screen showing **Resolved** or **Cancelled** until you change page. When nothing is pending, the card shows **Virtual client follow-ups (0 entries)**. See [Virtual clients](/releases/2026-11-09-virtual-clients).

### Pending Closure Requests

You see this card only if you can approve closure requests. Its columns are **PO Case**, **Virtual Client**, **Requested By**, **Reason**, **End Date** and **Status**. Rows you can approve have a **Review** button.

## Expanded Card Panels

Clicking any card opens an expanded panel below the cards. Click the **X** button, or click the same card again, to close it.

- **Entry Processing** — Shows <Term path="referral" plural /> from the **Intake** and **In Review** tabs in one list.
- **Awaiting Service** — The panel is titled **Awaiting Service Delivery**. It shows <Term path="referral" plural /> with status Service Review, Pending Allocation or Awaiting Service.
- **High Priority** — Shows <Term path="referral" plural /> with Urgent, Crisis or Emergency priority.

Each panel has its own **Search name or reference...** box, an **All Statuses** dropdown, and a **Clear** button once a filter is set. **Entry Processing** and **Awaiting Service** also have an **All Priorities** dropdown. **High Priority** does not, because it is already limited to high priorities.

The list is sorted with the newest first. Click a column heading to change that. Clicking a row opens the same summary window as on the tabs.

## Troubleshooting

### Dashboard Not Loading

1. Check your internet connection
2. Reload the page in your browser
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Clear your browser cache

### Missing <Term path="referral" plural /> {#missing-referrals}

If you can't find a <Term path="referral" />:
1. Check all four tabs (it may have moved to a different status). Completed and closed <Term path="referral" plural /> are not on any tab.
2. Use the search box with the name or reference number
3. Check the filters - you may have a filter active. Press **Clear** to reset the priority and date filters.
4. Go to the full <Term path="referral" plural /> page for advanced search

### Data Not Updating

The numbers on the cards and tab badges are not live. They are loaded when you open the page and kept for a few minutes, so reload the page if a number looks out of date.

1. Reload the page in your browser
2. Check if filters are affecting the view
3. Wait a few moments and try again
4. Contact support if the issue persists
