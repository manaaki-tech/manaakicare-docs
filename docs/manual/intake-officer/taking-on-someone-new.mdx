# Taking on someone new

**By the end of this page** you will have taken someone from a first enquiry
through to being accepted into a service, with a <Term path="caseWorker" />
assigned and their consent recorded.

This is the longest job in the system. It is one form in three steps, and the
system saves your place as you go.

<PictureWords />

## Before you start

Have these to hand:

- The person's **name** and **date of birth**
- Which **service** you are referring them to
- **Who referred them** — the agency or person
- **Whether they have consented**, and how

<Callout color="teal" title="Can't see the New Entry button?">
The button only appears for staff in an **intake** position, and for
**supervisors and managers**. <Term path="caseWorker" plural /> do
not see it — work reaches them already allocated. If you think you should have
it and don't, ask your administrator to check the positions on your services.
</Callout>

## Starting a new entry

Click **New Entry** at the top right of the screen.

<Screen

  caption="The menu down the left is always there. The New Entry button sits in the top bar, on the right."

The form opens on step 1 of 3. A bar across the top shows which step you are on.

## Step 1 — Who they are, and why

### Choose the service first

Pick the **service** from the list before you fill anything else in.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/01-choose-service.png"
  alt="The service dropdown open, showing available service contracts"
  caption="Choose the service first. It decides which extra questions appear further down the page — change it later and you will be answering them again."
  narrow
/>

### Find the person

Click the search box and start typing their name.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/02-search-for-someone.png"
  alt="Searching for a person by name, with matching results listed underneath"
  caption="Type two or three letters and wait. Matches appear underneath with their date of birth, so you can tell two people with the same name apart."
/>

- **If you find them** — click their name to select them.
- **If they are not there** — click **Create new client** and fill in the short
  form.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/03-add-a-new-person.png"
  alt="The form for adding a person who is not yet in the system"
  caption="You need their first name, last name, date of birth and gender. Anyone 16 or over also needs a phone, email or address. The rest can be filled in later."
/>

<Callout color="amber" title="Always search before you create">
Creating someone new here also creates a new whānau group for them. That makes
a duplicate more disruptive than it first looks — their history ends up split
across two records. Search first, even if you are fairly sure they are new.

If the person you pick already has other open <Term path="referral" plural />,
a warning appears once you select them. Read it before you carry on.
</Callout>

### Say why they are coming

Fill in the details of the enquiry itself — how urgent it is, when it came in,
how it reached you, and what it is about.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/04-entry-details.png"
  alt="The entry details section with seven numbered fields, from the client card down to the referrer's organisation"
  caption="Write the reason in plain words. It is the first thing the next person reads, and it shows at the top of their record from here on."
/>

Working down the numbers:

1. **Who it is about** — the person you picked a moment ago. **Change** swaps
   them if you have the wrong record.
2. **Referral type** — who made the enquiry: the person themselves, a family
   member, a school, or a professional.
3. **Urgency level** — this one matters. It sets how long you have before the
   first contact is overdue, shown under each option.
4. **Date of referral** — when it actually reached you, not the date you are
   typing it up.
5. **Channel** — how it arrived. Choose from the list, for example Email, Face to Face or Walk-in.
6. **Reason for referral** — a sentence or two, in plain words.
7. **Referrer contact** — for a school or professional referral, who referred them. Type a few letters of their name to
   search every organisation; if they are genuinely not there, choose **Add new
   referrer...**.

### Say who referred them

Search for the person who referred them in **Referrer contact**. Each result shows
their organisation, phone and city. If they are not listed, choose **Add new
referrer...** at the bottom of the list. Pick their organisation first, then add
their name and click **Create referrer**.

Underneath, you may see **extra questions specific to the service** you chose —
often about risk. These change from service to service.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/05-referrer-and-risk.png"
  alt="Referrer details above a set of service-specific risk questions"
  caption="The risk questions below the referrer belong to the service, not to the system. A different service asks different ones."
/>

When the page is complete, click **Continue**.

<Callout color="sage" title="This is the point where it is saved">
Clicking **Continue** at the end of step 1 creates the record. It gets a
reference number and sits as **Pending Consent**. You can also click
**Save Draft** part-way through step 1, once you have chosen the service and the
person. It then sits as a **Draft**. Either way, you can safely close the page
and come back to it later.

Until you click one of them, nothing is saved.
</Callout>

## Step 2 — Consent

### If they are under 16

Choose the parent or guardian who is giving consent in **Guardian**. If they are
not listed, choose **Add new guardian...** and add them. Then tick **Guardian
has consented** and fill in the **Guardian consent date**.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/06-add-a-guardian.png"
  alt="A form for adding a guardian, with name, relationship and phone number"
  caption="Choose how they are related from the list."
/>

### Record how consent was given

Say whether it was **verbal**, **written**, or **electronic**.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/07-how-consent-was-given.png"
  alt="Options for how consent was obtained, with a supporting documents section"
  caption="Verbal consent is allowed and is recorded as such. It does not stop you continuing — but the signed form will show up as a deadline later."
/>

### Attach the paperwork

If you have a signed form, upload it. Click **Upload**, choose the file, then
tell the system what kind of document it is.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/08-upload-a-document.png"
  alt="The upload dialog with the document type list open"
  caption="Choosing the right type matters — it is how the system knows whether a required document has been supplied."
  narrow
/>

### Tick what they have agreed to

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/09-consent-checklist.png"
  alt="A checklist of the things the person can consent to"
  caption="Tick only what they actually agreed to. Items marked with a red star must be ticked before you can continue."

## Step 3 — What happens to it

Click **Continue to Review** at the end of step 2. Check the summary, then click
**Submit Entry**. A window called **Send to team** opens.

This is the decision. You have three choices, and which one you pick depends on
how sure you are.

### Option 1 — Send for approval

**Use it when** you are not certain they meet the service's criteria, and you
want a manager to decide.

Choose the supervisor or manager who should look at it.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/10-send-for-approval.png"
  alt="The send-to-team dialog with the approval option selected and a supervisor chosen"
  caption="It leaves your hands and waits for them. Pick a real person — it does not go to a general queue."
  narrow
/>

### Option 2 — Accept

**Use it when** they clearly meet the criteria, but you are not ready to say who
will work with them.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/11-accept-the-entry.png"
  alt="The accept option selected, showing the care journey start date"
  caption="Check the start date. It defaults to today, and some of their deadlines are counted from it."
  narrow
/>

<Callout color="amber" title="Check the start date now">
Some deadlines are counted from this date. If the work actually started last
week, change it now — it can't be changed in the app afterwards.
</Callout>

### Option 3 — Accept and assign

**Use it when** you know both the service and the person who will do the work.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/12-accept-and-assign.png"
  alt="The accept-and-assign option with a staff member chosen from a list"
  caption="Assigning here saves a step later. The person you choose sees it on their dashboard straight away."
  narrow
/>

## Filling in the rest

Accepting someone does not mean their record is complete. Open them from your
list and work through the tabs when you have time.

### Intake info

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/14-intake-info.png"
  alt="The intake information tab of a person's record"
  caption="Everything from step 1, in one place, editable."
/>

### Family background

Cultural details are now recorded once for each person, not once for each
<Term path="referral" />. Open the care journey, go to **Entry Details**, then
**Whānau Background**, and select **Edit**. You fill in a grid:
**Are you Māori?**, then **Ethnicity**, then **Iwi**, **Hapū** and **Marae** if
the answer is Yes, then **Language**, **Spiritual belief** and **Religion**.
Ethnicity, Iwi, Hapū and Marae each have a **1st**, **2nd** and **3rd** box. The
others have one box.

Iwi and ethnicity matter for reporting as well as for the mahi. If you do not
know yet, leave it — do not guess. Choosing *Unknown* or *Not specified* is a
real answer, better than a guess that ends up in a report.

The steps, and what the grid will not accept, are in
[Cultural identity is now per person](/releases/2026-11-09-cultural-identity-per-person).

### Who else is around them

The support network is everyone else involved — whānau, and professionals from
other agencies.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/16-support-network.png"
  alt="The support network tab listing existing contacts"
  caption="People already in the system show as suggestions. Adding an existing person is better than typing them in again."
/>

To add someone new, click **Add Contact** and fill in the form.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/17-add-a-contact.png"
  alt="The support network section with three numbered areas: the section list, an existing contact, and the new contact form"
  caption="Say whether they are whānau or a professional. It changes how they are grouped."
/>

1. **Support Network** in the list down the left — where you are.
2. **People already added** — each one shows how they are related. The pencil
   edits them, the bin removes them.
3. **The new contact** — say whether they are whānau or a professional first, as
   it changes the questions underneath. Then their name and how they are related.

Click **Add to network** when you are done. Nothing is saved until you do.

To change someone already there, open them and edit.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/intake/18-edit-a-contact.png"
  alt="Editing an existing support network contact, with role checkboxes"
  caption="One person can hold more than one role. Tick all that apply rather than picking the closest."
/>

## If it does not look like this

- **No New Entry button** — you are probably in a
  <Term path="caseWorker" /> position, which does not have it. See the note at
  the top of this page.
- **The service list is empty** — you have not been given a position on any
  service. Your administrator fixes that.
- **The risk questions never appear** — you have not chosen a service yet.
  Choose it first and they load underneath.
- **You lost what you typed** — it had not been saved yet. On step 1, click
  **Save Draft** or **Continue** to save it.
