# Finishing up

**By the end of this page** you will be able to close someone's work properly,
with the right reason and date recorded.

<PictureWords />

## Before you close anything

Closing is not the same as pausing. If you expect to pick the work back up —
they are in hospital, away, or out of contact for now — use **Put on Hold**
instead.

Close only when the work has genuinely ended.

## Starting the closure

Open the person, find the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> card, and open its
actions menu.

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/journey/04-actions-menu.png"
  alt="The actions menu open, showing Put on Hold and Exit options"
  caption="Two options, and they are not alike. Put on Hold is easy to undo. Exit is not."
  narrow
/>

Choose the exit option.

## The closure form

<Screen
  src="/img/manual/closure/01-close-the-journey.png"
  alt="The closure dialog, with a reason, an end date, and a list of outstanding requirements"
  caption="Three things to get right: why it ended, when it ended, and what to do about anything still outstanding."

### Why it ended

Pick the reason that is actually true. This is what gets reported, and
"completed successfully" against work that stopped because the whānau
disengaged makes the numbers meaningless.

### When it ended

Put the real end date. If your last contact was three weeks ago, use that date
rather than today.

### Anything still outstanding

If required paperwork is missing, the form says so and offers to let you close
anyway.

<Callout color="amber" title="Overriding is a decision, not a shortcut">
You can close with paperwork missing, and sometimes that is the right call —
a whānau who has moved on is not going to come back and sign an evaluation
form.

But it is recorded as an override, and someone will see it. If there is a
reason, write it down.
</Callout>

## What closing does

- The <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is marked as ended.
- The linked <Term path="referral" /> is closed with it.
- The person stops appearing in your active list.
- Everything recorded stays. Nothing is deleted.

<Callout color="teal" title="They can come back">
Closing does not remove anyone. If they return, a new
<Term path="referral" /> is created and their history is still there — you are
not starting from nothing.
</Callout>

## If it does not look like this

- **There is no exit option** — you may not have permission to close work on
  this service. Ask a supervisor.
- **It will not let you pick an end date before the start date** — that is
  deliberate. If the start date is wrong, fix that first.
- **The closure documents are still locked** — they unlock once closure has
  begun. Start the closure, then upload them.
- **You closed it by mistake** — stop and ask a supervisor rather than creating
  a new record. Reopening is cleaner than a duplicate.
