# <Term path="activity" plural /> Overview

An <Term path="activity" /> is a record of a single piece of work done on a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> — a visit, a phone call, a coordination task, an administrative note, or an incident. <Term path="activity" plural /> live inside a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />'s **Care Journey** workspace, on its **Activities** tab, and are only ever attached to one <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> at a time.

## Where to find them

Open a client's Care Journey, then go to the **Activities** tab (or follow **View All Activities** from the Recent Activities card on the Care Journey overview). From there you can browse the activity timeline and open **+ New Activity** to record one.

## Activity types

When you create an <Term path="activity" />, the first thing you choose is its type. There are five:

| Type | What it's for | What it needs |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **<Term path="serviceUser" /> Visit** | In-person contact with the <Term path="serviceUser" />, at their home, your office, or elsewhere | A location and at least one participant |
| **Remote** | Contact that isn't in person — phone, video, email, SMS, or social media | A contact type |
| **Coordination** | Liaison or coordination work relating to the case that isn't direct contact | — |
| **Note / Admin** | Administrative work on the case | — |
| **Incident** | Recording a safety or other incident | Incident notes |

<Callout color="amber" title="The chip label follows your terminology">
The button you click for this type reads "<Term path="serviceUser" /> Visit" — it always uses whatever word your organisation has configured for "client", not a fixed label. The underlying record is the same either way.
</Callout>

Choosing a type sets which extra fields appear on the Essentials tab — see [Recording Visits](/activities/recording-visits) for the Visit walkthrough and [Phone Calls and Emails](/activities/phone-calls-and-emails) for Remote contact.

## Fields every activity has

Regardless of type, every <Term path="activity" /> has:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Free text, shown in the timeline |
| **Date & time** | Cannot be in the future, and cannot be more than 7 days in the past |
| **Duration** | Optional, in minutes |
| **Priority** | A risk-level rating on the <Term path="activity" /> itself — None, Low, Medium, High, or Critical. This is separate from the <Term path="referral" />'s urgency level |
| **Status (outcome)** | Completed, or a reason it didn't happen — see below |
| **Notes** | Rich-text notes, with optional templates — see [Case Notes](/activities/case-notes) |
| **Follow-up** | An optional required-follow-up flag with a date, up to three months out |

## When it didn't happen

If the <Term path="activity" /> didn't go ahead as planned, toggle **Didn't happen** on the Essentials tab and pick a reason: No-show, Unavailable, Not fit, Rescheduled, Client cancelled, or Staff cancelled.

## Saving

**Save Draft** stores what you have so far without requiring every field — useful if you're interrupted partway through. **Complete Activity** validates the form and saves it as a completed record. Both are available from the footer at any point after you've given the <Term path="activity" /> a title and a type.

Only the person who created an <Term path="activity" /> can edit it, and only within 10 days of creating it — after that, the Edit option no longer appears. See [Viewing Activity History](/activities/viewing-activity-history) for how to find and open past activities.
