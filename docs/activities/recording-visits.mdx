# Recording Visits

A **<Term path="serviceUser" /> Visit** <Term path="activity" /> documents in-person contact with a <Term path="serviceUser" /> — at their home, your office, in the community, or anywhere else you meet face to face. This page walks through creating one from start to finish; the same tabs and buttons apply to every <Term path="activity" /> type, so it also works as the general reference for the <Term path="activity" /> form.

## Before you start

You'll need the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> open — <Term path="activity" plural /> are created from a client's Care Journey, not from anywhere else.

## Starting a new activity

From the Care Journey's **Activities** tab, click **Log new**. This opens the <Term path="activity" /> form in the right-hand panel, with three tabs: **Essentials**, **Notes**, and **Follow-up**.

Give the <Term path="activity" /> a title at the top of the panel — this is what shows in the timeline, so make it identifiable (e.g. "Home visit — housing check-in").

## Essentials tab

### Activity type

Click the **<Term path="serviceUser" /> Visit** chip. This reveals the Location field and the Participants section further down the tab.

### When

Set the date and time, and optionally a duration in minutes. The date can't be in the future, and can't be more than 7 days in the past.

### Priority

Choose a risk-level rating for this specific <Term path="activity" />: None, Low, Medium, High, or Critical.

### Status

By default the <Term path="activity" /> is marked **Completed**. If the visit didn't go ahead as planned, click **Didn't happen** and pick a reason: No-show, Unavailable, Not fit, Rescheduled, Client cancelled, or Staff cancelled.

### Location

Pick where the visit took place: Home, Office, Community, Court, School, or Other.

### Participants

At least one participant is required for a <Term path="serviceUser" /> Visit. Click **Add participant**, then choose one of two sources:

- **Support Contact** — search the client's existing support network (whānau members and other support contacts already linked to the <Term path="referral" />), or add a new one. Adding a new whānau member needs a relationship and a phone number; adding a new professional support contact needs an organisation.
- **External** — search for a contact at any external organisation across the system, or add a new one by name and organisation.

<Callout color="teal" title="Participants aren't only for visits">
The Participants section appears for every <Term path="activity" /> type, not only <Term path="serviceUser" /> Visits — it's only required for this one.
</Callout>

## Notes tab

Write up what happened in the rich-text Notes field. See [Case Notes](/activities/case-notes) for using templates and what to include.

## Follow-up tab

Toggle **Follow-up required** if this visit needs a follow-up, then set a date. The date must be between today and three months from now.

## Saving

At the bottom of the form:

- **Save Draft** — saves your progress without validating every field. Use this if you're interrupted and need to come back later.
- **Complete Activity** — validates the form and saves it as a completed record.

If required fields are missing when you try **Complete Activity**, the form jumps to the first tab with an error.

<Callout color="amber" title="Editing window">
Once saved, only you (the creator) can edit the <Term path="activity" />, and only within 10 days of creating it. After that the Edit option disappears from the record.
</Callout>
