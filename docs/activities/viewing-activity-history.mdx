# Viewing Activity History

Every <Term path="activity" /> recorded against a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> appears in that Care Journey's activity timeline.

## Opening the timeline

From the Care Journey's **Activities** tab, click **Show timeline** to open the timeline panel alongside the <Term path="activity" /> you're viewing or editing. It's collapsed by default so the form has more room; click **Hide timeline** to close it again.

<Term path="activity" plural /> are grouped by date, newest first, and show the title, time, author, type, duration, and outcome (if it didn't happen as planned) at a glance.

## Searching and filtering

At the top of the timeline:

- **Search** matches against the <Term path="activity" /> title
- **Type** filters to one of four values: Direct, Coordination, Administrative, or Incident
- **Status** filters to Draft, Saved, or Archived

<Callout color="amber" title="Type filter is broader than it looks">
Both <Term path="serviceUser" /> Visit and Remote activities are stored as type **Direct** — the Type filter can't tell them apart. Filtering by "Direct" returns visits and remote contact together.
</Callout>

Search and filters apply only to activities already loaded into the timeline for this <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

## Opening an activity

Click any <Term path="activity" /> card to open it in the right-hand panel. This is a read-only view showing the notes, outcome, priority, visit or telehealth details, and follow-up information (if any), along with who created it and when.

## Editing an activity

An **Edit** button appears only if both of these are true:

- You created the <Term path="activity" />
- It was created within the last 10 days

Past that window, or for activities someone else created, there's no Edit option — the record is read-only from then on.
