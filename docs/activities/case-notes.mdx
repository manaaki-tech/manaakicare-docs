# Case Notes

<Callout color="teal" title="Notes aren't a separate activity type">
In Manaaki Central, there isn't a distinct "Case Note" <Term path="activity" /> — every <Term path="activity" />, whatever its type, has the same **Notes** tab. This page covers that tab.
</Callout>

## Writing notes

On the Notes tab, the main **Notes** field is a rich-text editor — use it to record what happened, what was observed, and what actions were taken. Below it, an **Additional Notes** field is available for any extra context that doesn't belong in the main write-up.

## Using templates

Click **Template** above the Notes field to insert a structured starting point instead of writing from scratch. Templates are grouped by where they come from:

- **From your service** — templates set up for the specific service this <Term path="activity" />'s <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> belongs to
- **Organisation** — templates available across all of your organisation's services

Search for a template by name, preview it before inserting, and click to insert — the template's content is appended to whatever is already in the Notes field, not used to replace it. If you insert the wrong one, an **Undo** option appears briefly afterward.

If your organisation hasn't set up any templates yet, the picker will say so — ask a service manager or administrator if you think you need one.

<Callout color="amber" title="No templates? Nothing to click">
The Template button always appears, but if your organisation or service has no templates configured, opening it just shows an empty-state message. This isn't a fault — templates are opt-in and set up per organisation.
</Callout>

## What to include

Good case notes are specific enough that someone else picking up the file later — a colleague, a supervisor, or an auditor — can understand what happened without needing to ask you. As a general guide:

- What was discussed or observed, in enough detail to stand alone
- Any decisions made or actions agreed, and who is responsible for them
- Anything relevant to risk, safety, or wellbeing
- Write notes as soon as practical after the <Term path="activity" /> took place, while it's fresh
