# Logging In

Manaaki Central uses **single sign-on with your Microsoft work account**. There is no separate Manaaki Central password to remember or reset.

## Signing in

1. Open your browser and go to your organisation's Manaaki Central address
2. Click **Sign In with Microsoft**
3. You are taken to Microsoft's sign-in page — enter your work account details there
4. Once Microsoft confirms who you are, you are returned to Manaaki Central and land on your dashboard

<Callout color="teal" title="Your password is managed by Microsoft, not Manaaki Central">
Because sign-in goes through your Microsoft work account, Manaaki Central never sees or stores your password. To change it, or if you have forgotten it, use your organisation's normal Microsoft account process — your administrator can help.
</Callout>

## Two-factor authentication

You will be asked to confirm your identity with the **Microsoft Authenticator** app when you:

- Sign in on a new device or browser for the first time
- Return after a long period without signing in
- Sign in from an unfamiliar location, depending on your organisation's policy

When prompted, open Microsoft Authenticator on your phone and approve the request, or enter the number shown on screen.

<Callout color="amber" title="Set up Authenticator before you need it">
If you have not set up Microsoft Authenticator yet, do it before your first sign-in on a new device — not while you are trying to start work. Your administrator can walk you through it.
</Callout>

## Session management

Sessions stay active while your browser is open. If you **sign out**, or the session expires through inactivity, you will need to sign in again.

If your session expires while you are working, you are redirected to the login page. After signing back in you are returned to the page you were on, so you do not lose your place.

:::note
On a shared or public computer, **sign out** and then close the browser when you finish to make sure your session ends.
:::

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **Sign In with Microsoft** does nothing | Wait a moment — the button shows **Initializing...** until it is ready. If it stays that way, refresh the page. |
| Microsoft accepts you but Manaaki Central does not | Your Microsoft account is not yet linked to a Manaaki Central user. Contact your organisation administrator. |
| No access to your Authenticator app | If your phone is lost, replaced, or unavailable, your administrator must reset your authentication method. There is no self-service route. |
| Account locked or disabled | Contact your organisation administrator. |
