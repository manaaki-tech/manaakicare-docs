# Understanding Your <Term path="dashboard" />

When you sign in, Manaaki Central shows you a dashboard matched to your role. Each dashboard is designed around the work that matters most to that role — so you can see what needs attention without wading through things that don't concern you.

There are three dashboards in the system. You will see the one that matches your role. If you hold more than one role, the most privileged dashboard is shown.

## Intake Officer <Term path="dashboard" /> {#intake-officer-dashboard}

The Intake Officer dashboard is a real-time pipeline view of all incoming entries moving through the system. Work is organised across four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|--------------|
| **Intake Processing** | Entries actively being worked on — drafts and in-progress intake steps |
| **In Review** | Entries that have completed the intake steps and are awaiting review |
| **Service Review** | Entries sent for service approval, awaiting a manager decision |
| **Awaiting Service Delivery** | Entries allocated and ready to begin, waiting for the <Term path="caseWorker" /> to activate |

New submissions and items needing attention surface at the top of each tab, so you can prioritise without manually scanning the queue.

## Supervisor / Programme Manager <Term path="dashboard" /> {#supervisor-programme-manager-dashboard}

The Supervisor dashboard gives you oversight of your team's work. It focuses on the decisions and checks that sit with your role:

- **Pending <Term path="referral" /> requests** — entries sent to you for approval or decline
- **Clients not recently contacted** — a prompt for <Term path="serviceUser" plural /> who may need a check-in
- **Pending service dispatches** — entries awaiting allocation to a <Term path="caseWorker" />

This view is designed to help you keep things moving across the team without needing to dig into individual records unnecessarily.

## <Term path="caseWorker" /> <Term path="dashboard" /> {#case-worker-dashboard}

The <Term path="caseWorker" /> dashboard is your personal caseload view. It brings together everything you need to manage your active clients:

- **Active <Term path="cases" />** — all <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> currently assigned to you
- **<Term path="activity" plural /> needing follow-up** — tasks or interactions that are due or overdue
- **Risk flags** — clients with elevated risk indicators requiring attention
- **Overdue follow-ups** — a direct list of anything past its expected follow-up date

The intent is that you can open Manaaki Central at the start of your day and immediately see where to focus.

:::tip
Each dashboard has its own detailed guide in the **Dashboards** section, including a walkthrough of every column, filter, and action available to your role.
:::

## Refreshing <Term path="dashboard" /> Data {#refreshing-dashboard-data}

Dashboard data updates automatically in the background. If you want to force a refresh — for example, after completing an action in another tab — use the refresh button in the header bar.
