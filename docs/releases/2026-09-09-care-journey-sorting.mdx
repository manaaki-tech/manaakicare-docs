# Sorting <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> by recent activity

*9 September 2026*

Your list of <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> now opens with the ones you
worked on most recently at the top, instead of the oldest or the most recently
created. There are also two new date columns you can sort by.

## What you'll notice first

Open either the <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> page or **My <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />**
on your <Term path="dashboard" />, and the list is sorted by **Last Activity**,
newest first — the journey you last recorded something against sits at the top.

Before this change, the <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> page opened sorted
by **Last Updated** and your <Term path="dashboard" /> list by when each
<Term path="serviceEpisode" /> was created — and **no column was marked as the
one being sorted on**, so there was no way to tell. Now the **Last Activity**
header carries a chevron on first load.

## Two new columns

| Column | What the date means |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Last Activity** | When an <Term path="activity" /> under this <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> was last created or edited |
| **Last Entry Change** | When particular <Term path="referral" /> details behind this <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> were last saved |

{/* SCREENSHOT NEEDED — 02-new-columns.png
    The right-hand end of the Care Journeys table showing the Last Activity and
    Last Entry Change columns side by side, with real dates in both and at least
    one row where they differ.
    Highlight: the two column headers. */}

<Callout color="amber" title="Last Entry Change is narrower than it sounds">
It does not move for every edit you make to the <Term path="referral" />. It is
updated when you save **Consent**, **Cultural Profile**, **Intake Assessment**, or
**Service Delivery Preferences** — and nothing else. Editing other parts of the
<Term path="referral" /> leaves this date where it was.
</Callout>

<Callout color="amber" title="Deleting an activity doesn't move the date back">
**Last Activity** is stamped when an <Term path="activity" /> is created or
edited. Deleting one afterwards leaves the date as it was, so a journey can show
a Last Activity date with nothing on its timeline to match.
</Callout>

## Sorting the list yourself

On the <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> page you can sort by **Client**,
**Start Date**, **Last Updated**, **Last Activity** and **Last Entry Change**.
Select a header once to sort by it, and again to reverse the direction.

**Service**, **Status**, **Assigned Staff** and **Actions** are not sortable —
selecting those headers does nothing.

While the re-sorted list loads, the rows you're already looking at stay on
screen, a thin progress line runs along the top of the table, and the arrow on
the column you selected spins. Previously the whole table — headers included —
was replaced by a fixed-height placeholder, so it visibly jumped as the real
rows arrived and the headers briefly stopped responding to clicks.

## Filtering by a date range

The same two dates are also available as filters on the
<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> page. Next to the search box is a dropdown
offering **Last Updated**, **Last Activity** and **Last Entry Change** — pick
which date you want to filter on, and a date-range picker appears beside it for
that date.

{/* SCREENSHOT NEEDED — 04-timestamp-filter.png
    The filter row on the Care Journeys page: the search box, the dropdown open
    showing its three options, and the date-range picker beside it.
    Highlight: the dropdown and the date-range picker together. */}

So "everything I haven't touched since August" is a date range on **Last
Activity**, and "everyone whose consent or assessment details changed last week"
is a date range on **Last Entry Change**.

## Where this doesn't apply

- **Completed Care** doesn't get the new columns and still opens in its previous
  order, by **Last Updated**. Sorting there did get the same fixes, though —
  selecting a header a second time now reverses the order properly, which it
  didn't before.
- If you do both intake and casework, your <Term path="dashboard" />'s
  **My <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />** table gets the new Last Activity
  order, but **its headers are not selectable** — you can read the order, not
  change it. Use the <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> page to sort.
- Supervisors and programme managers don't have this table on their
  <Term path="dashboard" /> at all, so nothing changes there.

<Callout color="teal" title="On a narrow screen the new columns are hidden">
**Last Activity** and **Last Entry Change** only appear on wide displays. On a
smaller laptop screen or a tablet the list is still *sorted* by Last Activity —
you just can't see the column it's sorting on.
</Callout>
