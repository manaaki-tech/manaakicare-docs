# Resolving a follow-up

*1 September 2026*

On the dashboard's **Activities Needing Follow-up** table, you can now resolve a
follow-up directly from the row — recording what happened without opening a new
<Term path="activity" />.

## Resolving from the dashboard

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution/01-dashboard-tick-and-menu.png"
  alt="The Activities Needing Follow-up table on the dashboard, with the tick button and the 3-dot menu highlighted at the right of a row"
  caption="Each row in the follow-up table has two actions on the right: a tick to resolve it, and a 3-dot menu for other options."
/>

Selecting the tick opens a panel asking for the title of the completion
<Term path="activity" />, prefilled for you:

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution/02-resolve-panel-completed.png"
  alt="The resolve panel open under a follow-up row, showing an editable title, Completed and Didn't happen buttons, a Needs another follow-up checkbox, and Save & resolve"
  caption={<>1&nbsp;— the title, editable and prefilled. 2&nbsp;— Completed or Didn't happen. 3&nbsp;— Needs another follow-up. 4&nbsp;— Save &amp; resolve.</>}
/>

<Callout color="amber" title="This creates an activity">
Resolving or closing a follow-up uses the details in the resolution panel to
create an <Term path="activity" />, which you'll see on the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />'s
Care Journey timeline.
</Callout>

The title is prefilled with a prefix of **Completed** or **Didn't happen**, and you
can edit it before saving. In Manaaki Central, a **Completed** follow-up is like
saying "resolved", whereas **Didn't happen** means "closed".

## Didn't happen, and rescheduling

Choosing **Didn't happen** asks why it didn't happen, and lets you set a new
follow-up date in the same step:

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution/03-resolve-panel-didnt-happen.png"
  alt="The resolve panel with Didn't happen selected, showing reason buttons (No-show, Unavailable, Not fit, Rescheduled, Client cancelled, Staff cancelled) and a Needs another follow-up date field"
  caption={<>1&nbsp;— Didn't happen. 2&nbsp;— the reason, here Rescheduled. 3&nbsp;— Needs another follow-up, with a new date.</>}
/>

Check **Needs another follow-up** and set a date to create the next one at the same
time — this is how you reschedule.

Here's what that looks like afterwards, from the <Term path="activity" /> timeline —
a follow-up closed as **Didn't happen**, with **Not fit** as the reason and no
further follow-up:

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution/04-timeline-didnt-happen.png"
  alt="The activity timeline showing a Didn't happen — Update from Medical Centre entry, with a banner reading 'Activity recorded as: Client Not Fit'"
/>

**Completed** and **Didn't happen** should cover most of what you need — even
something like "follow-up created by accident" is worth documenting this way.

## Clearing a follow-up with nothing left to add

Sometimes the case notes have moved on far enough that there's no useful follow-up
detail left to record. For those, clear the follow-up instead: open the 3-dot menu
on the row and choose **No longer needed**.

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution/05-menu-log-activity-no-longer-needed.png"
  alt="The 3-dot menu open on a follow-up row, showing Log activity and No longer needed options"
/>

A confirmation lets you back out before it's cleared:

<Screen
  src="/img/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution/06-cancel-follow-up-confirm.png"
  alt="A confirmation dialog: 'Cancel Follow-up? Are you sure this follow-up is no longer needed? This will mark it as canceled and remove it from your follow-up list.' with Keep Follow-up and Yes, Cancel Follow-up buttons"
  narrow
/>

Unlike **Completed** or **Didn't happen**, this does not create an <Term path="activity" /> —
it just removes the follow-up.

## Logging a full activity instead

The **Log activity** option, just above **No longer needed** in the same menu, closes
the follow-up too — but by capturing the full set of details you'd normally use when
creating an <Term path="activity" />: the familiar **Essentials**, **Notes**, and
**Follow-up** tabs.
