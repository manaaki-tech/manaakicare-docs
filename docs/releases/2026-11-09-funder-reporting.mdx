# Funder reporting on care journeys

*9 November 2026*

If your service reports to a funder, the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> now
shows what the funder needs, what is still missing, and what must be sorted out
before you close. You can fill it in from the **Entry Details** tab, from the
**Overview** tab, or from a chip in the header.

<Callout color="amber" title="You will only see this on some services">
Everything on this page appears only when your service's contract has funder
fields set up. If your <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> shows none of it, that is
expected. Ask your administrator whether your service's contract has been set up
for funder reporting.
</Callout>

## Service details

Open the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> and go to **Entry Details**. There is a
new section called **Service details**, followed by your service's name.

The questions are grouped under three headings:

- **Start of journey**
- **Confirmed with funder**
- **During the journey**

The section is one form. A count at the top reads **n of m complete**, for
example "3 of 7 complete", so you can see how much is left.

At the bottom are **Save** and **Cancel**. **Save** stays greyed out until you
change something. **Cancel** discards what you have typed.

Funder questions no longer appear in **Intake Info**. In their place, **Intake
Info** points you to **Service details**.

## The Funder reporting chip

In the header of the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> there is a new chip that
begins **Funder reporting**. Select it to open a small panel with two parts:

- **Needed before closure**
- **Quick update**

When nothing is outstanding, the panel says **All caught up — nothing the
funder needs right now.**

## The Overview tab

On the **Overview** tab, the **Service data** card changes for services with
funder fields. It now shows:

- a status badge on each item
- editing in place, without leaving the Overview tab
- a link at the bottom, **Full record in Entry Details → Service details**, that
  opens the whole form

## What the statuses mean

Each row shows one of these:

| You will see | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Needed now** | The funder needs this now |
| **Blocks closure** | This must be dealt with before you close the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />. It is also listed in the Exit dialog (see below) |
| **Needed from 12 Dec 2026** | This is not needed yet. The date shown is when it will be (the date here is only an example) |
| **Waiting on a reply** | The item is waiting on a reply |
| **Updated 5 Nov 2026** | It has been filled in. The date is the day it was last updated (example date) |

### Items that appear later

Some items only apply once a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> has been open for a
set number of days. Until then they stay hidden. They appear once the
number of days has passed.

## Clearing a field, and Undo

When you edit a funder field you now get a **Clear** action.

On fields where picking an option saves straight away, **Clear** also saves
straight away. A message appears: **Cleared** followed by the field name, with
an **Undo** button. Select **Undo** to put the answer back.

Fields that allow several choices show each choice as a small removable tag.

## Closing a care journey

When you choose **Exit <Term path="serviceEpisode" />**, the dialog lists the
items that are blocking closure. This list now also includes funder items marked
**Blocks closure** that have not been filled in. Items that do not apply yet
(see "Items that appear later") are left out.

<Callout color="amber" title="Check the Exit dialog, not just the badge">
This page can only tell you what the Exit dialog shows: the list of blockers. It
does not promise that closure is stopped in every case. Treat **Blocks closure**
as a clear instruction to fill the item in first, and read the Exit dialog before
you go ahead. For the rest of the exit steps, see
[Closing a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />](/care-journeys/closing-an-episode).
</Callout>

## Better dropdowns

The dropdowns on dynamic forms are easier to use:

- A dropdown with more than four options now has a search box. Type to find an
  option.
- Where you can pick several options, they are grouped under headings that stay
  in view as you scroll. An **i** icon lists everything you have picked.
- Options such as **Not yet confirmed** sit apart from the main list, so you can
  find them without scrolling.
- Where there are only two choices, they still sit side by side as two cards.

## For administrators: renaming Entry Details

An organisation can rename the **Entry Details** tab, and the matching link at
the bottom of the Overview card. The default name is **Entry Details**. If yours
shows a different name, use that name wherever this page says **Entry Details**.
Ask your administrator if you want it changed.

## If your screen does not match this page

First, check whether your service has funder reporting at all. If there is no
**Service details** section and no **Funder reporting** chip, your service's
contract probably has no funder fields set up. That is not a fault. Ask your
administrator.

If your service does have it and something looks different, take a screenshot
and send it to your administrator, along with the name of the service and the
<Term path="serviceEpisode" /> you were on. Please do not guess at an answer
for a field you are unsure about. Leave it blank and ask.
