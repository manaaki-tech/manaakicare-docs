# Faster lists: filters, sorting and search

*9 November 2026*

Three lists have been rebuilt: the <Term path="referral" plural /> list, the
<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> list, and the **Completed Care** page.
By the end of this page you will know how to filter each one, how to sort it,
and how the search box behaves.

<Callout color="amber" title="This replaces the older table">
The earlier release note,
[Sorting <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> by recent activity](/releases/2026-09-09-care-journey-sorting),
describes the table as it was before this change. Where the two pages differ,
trust this one.
</Callout>

Your organisation can rename the menu items and the words
<Term path="referral" plural /> and <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />. This
page uses your organisation's words wherever it can. Button, tab and column
names that are shown in **bold** are quoted as they appear on screen.

## The referrals list

The old filter bar has gone. You now sort and filter from the column headings,
much as you would in a spreadsheet.

What you will see:

- Three tabs: **Drafts**, **Active** and **Closed**. Each shows a badge with its total.
- A search box next to the page title. It reads **Search entries...**
- Filters for **Status**, **Priority**, **Service** and a date range for when the <Term path="referral" /> was created.
- A footer that reads **Showing X-Y of N**, where N is the total in the list.
- The safety-alert icon, shown in the **Client** cell of any row that has an alert.
- The **Referral #** column is hidden by default.

You can pick more than one service at a time. You can also sort by client name.

<Callout color="teal" title="Your filters and sort are remembered">
The filters and sort you choose stay in the page address, and they are
remembered for you personally. When you come back to the list, it opens the way
you left it. If a list looks shorter than you expect, check whether a filter
is still switched on.
</Callout>

## The care journeys list

This list has been rebuilt, and it shows 20 rows per page. The number of matching rows appears beside the page title.

### Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| **All open** | Every open <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> |
| **Active** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> currently delivering service |
| **Awaiting Staff** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> with no <Term path="caseWorker" /> assigned yet |
| **Awaiting Commencement** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> with staff assigned, not yet commenced |
| **On Hold** | Paused <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> |

The **Awaiting Staff** tab is hidden from <Term path="caseWorker" plural />. If
you are a <Term path="caseWorker" />, you will not see it.

### Filters and columns

| Where | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| **Risk** column | New. Filter by **None**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Critical** |
| **Service** filter | Pick more than one service at once |
| **Assigned Staff** filter | Managers and supervisors only. Other roles do not see it |
| **Status** column | Shown on the **All open** tab only |

### Sorting and reloading

Select a column heading to sort by it. The new sort replaces the old one. Sorts
do not stack on top of each other.

While the list reloads after a sort, filter or search, the rows fade.

### Recipe: high-risk journeys that need staff

1. Select the **Awaiting Staff** tab.
2. Open the filter on the **Risk** column.
3. Tick **High**.
4. Tick **Critical**.

The rows fade, then the list shows only the matching rows. The count beside the
title updates to match.

If you are a <Term path="caseWorker" />, you cannot see the **Awaiting Staff**
tab. Use the **All open** tab for this recipe instead.

## Completed Care

The **Completed Care** page uses the same table as the
<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> list.

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| **All** | Everything on the page |
| **Closed** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that were closed |
| **Completed** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> that were completed |

There are two new columns:

- **End Date**. You can sort by it, and filter it by a date range.
- **Closure Reason**. You can filter it by more than one reason at a time.

There are also **Service** and **Assigned Staff** filters.

### Recipe: closed journeys that ended in October

1. Select the **Closed** tab.
2. Open the filter on the **End Date** column.
3. Choose 1 October as the start of the range.
4. Choose 31 October as the end of the range.

The list reloads and shows only <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> whose end
date falls in that range. To narrow it further, open the **Closure Reason**
filter and tick the reasons you want.

## Searching

On the <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> list and the **Completed Care** page, the search box works like this:

- It starts searching after you have typed **3 letters** and paused briefly.
- If you want to search for fewer letters, type them and press **Enter**.
- The hint reads **Press Enter to search, or type 3 or more letters**.
- The **x** button, labelled **Clear search**, empties the box and clears the search. Pressing **Esc** does the same.

The box on the <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> list reads
**Search client, service or staff name...**. The box on **Completed Care** reads
**Search completed** followed by your organisation's word for
<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />.

## If your screen does not match this page

- **A tab, column or filter is missing.** **Awaiting Staff** is hidden from <Term path="caseWorker" plural />. **Assigned Staff** is only for managers and supervisors. **Status** is only on the **All open** tab.
- **The list is shorter than you expect.** A filter from your last visit may still be on, because your filters are remembered. Check the filters in the column headings.
- **Search does nothing.** You may have typed fewer than 3 letters. Press **Enter**, or type another letter.
- **A name looks different.** Your organisation can rename menu items and the words used for <Term path="referral" plural /> and <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />.

If your screen still does not match, tell your supervisor or administrator what
you see, and which list you were on. Do not work around it.
