# Cultural identity now belongs to the person

*9 November 2026*

You now record someone's cultural identity once, and it follows them. Ethnicity,
iwi, hapū, marae, language, spiritual belief and religion are held on the
person, not on each <Term path="referral" />. By the end of this page you will
know where to find them, how to fill in the new grid, and what it will not
accept.

## What changed

Until now, cultural details were saved separately on every
<Term path="referral" />. If someone came back for a second service, you
entered them again.

Now there is **one record per person**. It is shared across all of their
<Term path="referral" plural /> and <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />. Fill
it in once, and everyone working with that person sees the same answers.

## Where to find it

There are two places:

- **In a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />:** open **Entry Details**, then
  **Whānau Background**. This shows a read-only summary with an edit pencil.
- **On the person's Overview:** a new **Cultural Identity** card. It says
  *Shared across all referrals and care journeys for this person.*

## Filling in the grid

The grid is laid out like the paper form, so it should feel familiar.

1. Open **Entry Details**, then **Whānau Background**.
2. Select the edit pencil.
3. Answer **Are you Māori?** with **Yes** or **No**.
4. In the **Ethnicity** row, choose an answer in the **1st** box.
5. If the person has more than one, choose the next answer in the **2nd** box.
6. If you need more boxes than the row shows, select **+ more**.
7. If a value is not in the list, choose **Other...** and type it in your own words.
8. If you don't know, choose **Unknown**. If the person chose not to say, choose **Not specified**.
9. To add a note to one answer, use the note beside its box.
10. Repeat for **Language**, **Spiritual belief** and **Religion**.
11. If you answered **Yes** to **Are you Māori?**, fill in **Iwi**, **Hapū** and **Marae** the same way.
12. Save your changes.

When a value is already used in another box on the same row, the grid marks it
**Already 1st**, so you can see where it went.

<Callout color="amber" title="Iwi, Hapū and Marae only appear for Māori">
The **Iwi**, **Hapū** and **Marae** rows show only when **Are you Māori?** is
**Yes**. If the answer is **No**, they are not there to fill in.
</Callout>

## What the grid will not accept

If something is wrong, an error appears and the page scrolls to it. The rules
are:

- **No iwi, hapū or marae unless the person is Māori.**
- **A Māori person needs at least one ethnicity.** Answering **Yes** and leaving
  **Ethnicity** empty will not save.
- **One main answer per row.** A row can have only one first choice.
- **No repeats.** The same value cannot appear twice in a row.

## Compliance

The cultural identity requirement is now checked from the person's record, not
from each <Term path="referral" />. A person's cultural identity counts as
complete when they have an ethnicity, or when they are Māori and have an iwi.
Once it is complete, it covers every <Term path="referral" /> for that person.

## What has gone

- **The three old sub-sections.** **Māori Identity**, **Language & Beliefs** and
  **Cultural Practices** no longer appear on a <Term path="referral" />. The
  **Whānau Background** grid replaces them.
- **Cultural Identity badges in the referral preview.** The preview window
  that opens from a <Term path="referral" /> no longer shows them. Look in
  **Whānau Background** or on the person's Overview instead.

## Who this affects

Anyone who enters or reads cultural details. If you have already entered them
for a person on one <Term path="referral" />, check **Whānau Background**
before you enter them again.

<Callout color="amber" title="Check cultural details you entered before">
We have not been able to confirm what happens to cultural details that were
saved on individual <Term path="referral" plural /> before this change. After
launch, open a few people you know well and check that their details are there.
If something you recorded earlier is missing, ask your administrator.
</Callout>

## If your screen does not match this page

Your organisation may have set things up differently, or the lists in the grid
may look different to the ones described here. If the grid has other rows, or
**Whānau Background** is not where this page says, tell your administrator or
supervisor before you change anything. Do not guess at an answer. Leaving a
row as **Unknown** or **Not specified** is better than a wrong one.
