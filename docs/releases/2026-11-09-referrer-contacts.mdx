# Finding and adding referrer contacts

*9 November 2026*

When you record who made a <Term path="referral" />, you can now search for the
referrer contact across every organisation at once. You no longer pick the
organisation first. This page also covers adding a new referrer and a few
smaller changes around the same screens.

<Callout color="amber" title="The old order has gone">
Earlier pages and training notes may tell you to choose the organisation, then
the contact. That step is gone from the **Referrer contact** field. If a guide
tells you to do it, the guide is out of date, not your screen.
</Callout>

## Search for a referrer contact

On the intake form, professional and educational <Term path="referral" plural />
show a **Referrer contact** field.

1. Click the **Referrer contact** field.
2. Type a name. The field says **Search for a referrer contact...**
3. Click the person you want in the results.

Results come from all organisations. Each one shows the organisation name, the
phone number and the city. Use these to tell two people with the same name apart.

### What the search matches

The search looks at more than the person's name. It now also matches:

- the referrer's secondary phone number
- the organisation's phone, email, first address line, suburb and city

So you can find a contact by typing something you know about their organisation.
The search for an external organisation also matches phone and email.

## Add a new referrer

If the person is not in the results, you can add them.

1. At the bottom of the results, choose **Add new referrer...**. A **New Referrer** form opens.
2. Choose the **Organisation**. It is marked with an asterisk because it is required.
3. Fill in **First name** and **Last name**. They appear once you have chosen the organisation. **Phone** and **Email** are optional.
4. Choose **Create referrer**.

**Create referrer** stays greyed out until you have chosen an organisation and
typed a first and last name. If you cannot click it, check the organisation
first.

When you choose the organisation, the phone number fills in from that
organisation. You do not need to type it.

## Phone and email are no longer required

A referrer from a source no longer needs a phone number or an email. This means
you can save a professional whose source has no contact details on file.

A phone number is still needed in one case. When you add a support contact who
is whānau or family, a phone number is required. Other support contacts do not
need one.

<Callout color="amber" title="Check for a phone number yourself">
Because the system no longer insists on a phone or email for a referrer, an
empty field will not stop you saving. If your team needs a way to reach the
referrer, add it yourself.
</Callout>

## Support contacts in the care journey

Support contacts in the care journey use the same selector as above. The card that used to be titled
**Organisation & contact** is now titled **Referrer contact**.

## Editing a referrer no longer makes a duplicate

Before this release, changing the referrer on an entry's intake details could
create a second copy of that referrer. This is fixed. Editing the referrer in
**Entry Details**, under **Intake Info**, now updates the one that already exists.

## A small change on the client summary

On the client summary card in the referral form, the small link icon beside
**Change** has been removed. **Change** is still there and works as before.

## If your screen does not match this page

- **You still have to pick an organisation before the contact** — you may be
  looking at an older page or a part of the system that has not updated. Refresh
  the page and try again.
- **Create referrer will not click** — check that you have chosen an
  **Organisation** and typed a first and last name. The organisation must be
  chosen first.
- **A phone number is asked for and you have none** — it is only needed for
  whānau or family support contacts. For a professional, check the contact type.
- **The card is still called Organisation & contact** — refresh the page and
  check again.

If it still looks different, ask your administrator before you carry on. Tell
them what you see on screen and which page you were on.
