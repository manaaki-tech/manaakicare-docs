# Smaller changes since September

*9 November 2026*

**By the end of this page** you will know which small changes have arrived since
12 September, whether each one affects you, and what to do about it.

Some of these have been live for weeks and are only written up now. Each item
says either **Live since** a date, or **New in this release**.

<Callout color="amber" title="Safety alert permissions have changed">
Who can raise an alert, and who can decide on one, now depends on your
position on the service. The **Administrator** role no longer overrides it.
See [Safety alerts](#safety-alerts) below.
</Callout>

## Dashboards

| What changed | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Live since 25 September.** A new **<Term path="activity" plural /> Needing Follow-up** table. Columns: **Reference**, **Client**, **Service**, **Follow-up Date**, **Priority**. | Intake Officer <Term path="dashboard" />, and Supervisor / Manager <Term path="dashboard" /> | Resolve the follow-up from its row, without leaving the dashboard. Steps are in [Resolving a follow-up](/releases/2026-09-01-follow-up-resolution). |
| **Live since 25 September.** Managers of more than one service see that table on the **Overview** tab only, not on the **Services** tab. | Managers of more than one service | Look on the **Overview** tab. |
| **Live since 25 September.** Most <Term path="dashboard" /> tables now shrink to their heading plus **(0 entries)** when they are empty, for example **<Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> (0 entries)** or the **Activities Needing Follow-up (0 entries)** table on the Intake Officer and Supervisor / Manager dashboards. The empty-state icon and message are gone. | Everyone | Read the heading. **(0 entries)** means nothing is waiting. The <Term path="caseWorker" /> follow-up table is the exception: it still shows **No follow-ups due**. |
| **Live since 25 September.** The **<Term path="serviceUser" plural /> Needing Contact** table has been removed. | Supervisor / Manager <Term path="dashboard" /> | Nothing to do. You will no longer see it. |

When nothing is due on the new follow-up table, it is hidden, apart from that
heading.

## Compliance panel on a care journey

**New in this release.**

| What changed | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| The narrow compliance bar at the side of a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> now opens the full panel as a sheet on the right, titled **Compliance**. | Anyone who can see compliance on a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> | Open it to see everything required for this person. |
| Hovering over the narrow bar shows a short summary: a line such as **3 of 5 on track**, counts of overdue, due soon and pending items, and the top items. | Same | Hover to check at a glance. You do not need to open the sheet. |
| The sheet has a **Pin panel** button. It docks the panel on the page. **Unpin panel** puts it back. | Same | Press **Pin panel** to keep it open while you work. |
| On screens narrower than 1536 pixels, pinning the panel collapses the app's side menu to make room. | Same | Press **Unpin panel** to get the menu back. |
| The Overview column no longer leaves a blank gap when the compliance bar is missing or collapsed. It is now centred. | Same | Nothing to do. |

Before this release the narrow bar had buttons named **Pin compliance panel**
and **Expand compliance panel**. The guides
[Staying on top of deadlines](/manual/case-worker/staying-on-top-of-deadlines)
and [Working with someone](/manual/case-worker/working-with-someone) now
describe the new behaviour. The pictures in them were taken before this release.

## Safety alerts

Changes to who can raise and decide on alerts have been live since 21 to 27
September. The two fixes at the end are new in this release. The full steps
are on the [Safety alerts](/manual/everyone/safety-alerts) page.

| What changed | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Live since 21 to 27 September.** Managers, supervisors and intake officers can raise an alert on any <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> under a contract they staff. They do not need to be assigned to it. | Those three positions | Raise the alert from the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />. Everyone else still needs to be assigned first. |
| **Live since 21 to 27 September.** The raise form now has up to two buttons. **Raise and approve** appears if you are a manager or supervisor on the service that owns the alert. **Ask another manager to approve** sits beside it, and appears only when there is someone else who could approve. Everyone else sees **Request approval**. | Anyone who raises an alert | Choose the button that matches what you want to happen next. |
| **Live since 21 to 27 September.** Supervisors and managers see their own alerts and the lists of alerts waiting for a decision only for services where they are active staff. | Supervisors and managers | If a service's alerts are missing, check you are active staff on it. |
| **Live since 21 to 27 September.** The right to confirm, resolve, cancel, edit, and decide on access requests comes only from your position on the service the alert was raised on. Administrator accounts no longer override it, apart from system administrators. | Everyone | If you cannot act on an alert, ask someone with the right position on that service. |
| **Live since 21 to 27 September.** The alert's details show the name of the person who created it. | Anyone who can open the alert in full | Nothing to do. |
| **Live since 17 September.** The "has alert" flag on <Term path="dashboard" /> and <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> lists shows only when that <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> itself has an active alert. | Everyone | Nothing to do. |
| **New in this release.** **View Care Journey** in the alert review window now opens the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> the alert was raised on. | Anyone reviewing an alert | Nothing to do. |
| **New in this release.** A person-level alert with no category now shows the hover text **Safety alert**. It used to read **Safety alert: undefined**. | Everyone | Nothing to do. |

## On Hold can be switched off for a service

**New in this release.**

| What changed | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Each service now has an **Allow on hold** setting. It is on by default. When it is off, setting a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> to On Hold is refused with the message **On Hold is not enabled for this service.** | Anyone who tries to put a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> on hold, on a service where it is off | Do not try to hold it. Ask your administrator if you think the setting is wrong. |

## How things look

**New in this release.**

| What changed | Who sees it | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Hint text in form fields (the grey words that show before you type) is now plain muted grey. It is no longer italic or faded. | Everyone | Nothing to do. |
| Loading placeholders are now neutral grey instead of teal. | Everyone | Nothing to do. |

## If your screen does not match this page

Some changes depend on your position, your service or your organisation's
settings, so you may not see every item. If something here is missing from your
screen, or your screen shows something this page does not mention, tell your
supervisor or administrator what you see and where. Do not work around it.
