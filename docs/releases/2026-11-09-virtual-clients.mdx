# Virtual clients for Police referrals

*9 November 2026*

If your service receives Police referrals, you can now record the people named
in a Police email without needing their names. Each person becomes a **virtual
client**: a placeholder holding only a date of birth, gender and address. When
a person agrees to work with you, the virtual client is linked to their
<Term path="referral" />, and the work you recorded carries across to their care
journey.

A virtual client never holds a name. Names stay in the Police email.

<Callout color="amber" title="Only on some services">
Not every service uses virtual clients. If yours does not, you will see no
**Virtual Clients** item in the left menu, and nothing on this page applies to
you.
</Callout>

## What is new at a glance

| Where | What you can do | Full steps |
|--------|-----------------|------------|
| **Virtual Clients** in the left menu | Register a PO case from a Police email and add each person on it | [Register a PO case and add people](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-register-and-add-people) |
| A PO case or virtual client page | Write notes for several people at once, and set follow-ups | [Notes and follow-ups](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-notes-and-follow-ups) |
| A person's menu on a PO case | Request closure, withdraw a request, or fix a wrong link | [Closing and fixing mistakes](/manual/case-worker/virtual-clients-closing-and-fixing) |
| **New Entry** | Link a virtual client to a <Term path="referral" /> in the **Virtual Client (Optional)** card | [Linking a virtual client](/manual/intake-officer/linking-a-virtual-client) |
| Your <Term path="dashboard" /> | See **Virtual client follow-ups** and, if you approve closures, **Pending Closure Requests** | [Closures and consent](/manual/supervisor/virtual-clients-closures-and-consent) |

For the whole picture, read the [virtual clients overview](/virtual-clients/overview).

## What changes for you

- **<Term path="caseWorker" plural />** register PO cases, add people, write notes, set follow-ups and request closures.
- **Intake officers** link a virtual client to a <Term path="referral" /> in **New Entry**. After linking, a check compares the Police record with the person's record, and you resolve any differences before you can continue.
- **Accepting the <Term path="referral" />** records consent and starts the care journey. Most services allocate directly: the intake officer chooses **Accept & assign Case Worker** when they submit. If the entry is sent for approval instead, a supervisor or manager accepts it.
- **Supervisors and managers** also review closure requests.

## Things to know

<Callout color="amber" title="Some limits to plan around">
- There is no way to merge two virtual clients for the same person. A wrong link is fixed one person, on one PO case, at a time.
- Nobody is notified when a person is assigned, relinked, or a closure is approved or rejected. Tell the person yourself.
- Approving a closure is final. A new incident is a new PO case.
- Once a referral is accepted, the virtual client is locked to it and can no longer be changed or cleared.
- The status filter on the Virtual Clients list has no **Closure requested** option.
</Callout>

## If your screen does not match this page

If the **Virtual Clients** menu item is missing, your service may not use
virtual clients, or you may not be staff on it. Ask your administrator. Closure
options also depend on your position in the service, and a change to who may
close directly can take up to 24 hours to apply.

If something else looks different, tell your administrator.
