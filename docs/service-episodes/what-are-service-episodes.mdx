# What are <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />?

A <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is the delivery record for a client — it exists for as long as a <Term path="caseWorker" /> is actively working with them under a service, and it's where <Term path="activity" plural /> get recorded against.

## <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> vs <Term path="referral" /> {#service-episode-vs-referral}

<Callout color="teal" title="Two different records">
The <Term path="referral" /> is the intake record — the request for service, filled in during the three-step wizard. The <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is the delivery record, created once the <Term path="referral" /> is accepted into a service. They have separate statuses and separate lifecycles, but both stay linked to the same client for the life of the episode.
</Callout>

Everything before allocation — intake, consent, dispatch, approval — happens on the <Term path="referral" />. Everything from allocation onward — assigning staff, delivering the service, recording visits and notes, eventually closing out — happens on the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.

## How a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> comes to exist {#how-a-service-episode-comes-to-exist}

You don't create a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> directly — there's no "New Episode" button. One is created automatically the moment a <Term path="referral" /> is accepted into a service, whether that's:

- A direct **Accept entry** or **Accept & assign kaimahi** at dispatch, or
- A supervisor or programme manager **accepting** a service request sent for approval

See [Submitting for Review](/referrals/submitting-for-review) for the full dispatch and approval flow, and [Starting an Episode](/service-episodes/starting-an-episode) for what happens next.

## Lifecycle at a glance

A <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> moves through up to six statuses:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Awaiting Staff Assignment** | Accepted into a service, but no <Term path="caseWorker" /> confirmed yet |
| **Awaiting Commencement** | <Term path="caseWorker" /> assigned, waiting for them to commence |
| **Active** | Service delivery is underway |
| **On Hold** | Delivery temporarily paused |
| **Completed** | Concluded successfully |
| **Closed** | Ended without full completion |

**Completed** and **Closed** are both final — once an episode reaches either one, it cannot be reopened or changed. For the full transition rules and a diagram, see the [Workflow Overview](/getting-started/workflow-overview#service-episode-statuses).

## Where you'll work: the Care Journey workspace

Opening a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> takes you to its **Care Journey** workspace — one place with everything related to that client's episode:

| Tab | What's there |
|-----|---------------|
| **Overview** | Status, assigned staff, recent activity, and the actions available for the episode's current status |
| **Entry Details** | The intake information carried over from the <Term path="referral" />, plus service-specific assessments |
| **Activities** | Every recorded visit, call, and note against this episode |
| **Documents** | Files attached to the client's record |
