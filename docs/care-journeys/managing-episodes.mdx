# Managing <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />

Learn how to find your <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />, manage assigned staff, and change an episode's status while it's open.

## Finding your episodes

Open **In-progress Journeys** from the sidebar to see everything in progress. (Your organisation can rename this menu item.) The page is organised into tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| **All open** | Every open episode |
| **Active** | Episodes currently delivering service |
| **Awaiting Staff** | Episodes with no <Term path="caseWorker" /> assigned yet. <Term path="caseWorker" plural /> don't see this tab. |
| **Awaiting Commencement** | Episodes with staff assigned, not yet commenced |
| **On Hold** | Paused episodes |

<Callout color="teal" title="Closed episodes have their own page">
**Completed** and **Closed** episodes don't appear here — they live on the **Completed Care** page instead. See [Closing an Episode](/care-journeys/closing-an-episode).
</Callout>

Search by client, service or staff name, and click a row (or the eye icon at its end) to open an episode's Care Journey workspace. The search starts after you have typed 3 letters and paused. To search for fewer letters, type them and press **Enter**. You can also filter by **Risk**, by **Service** (more than one at once) and, if you are a manager or supervisor, by **Assigned Staff**. The full list of tabs, filters and columns is in [Lists, filters and search](/releases/2026-11-09-lists-filters-and-search).

### How the list is ordered

The list opens sorted by **Last Activity**, newest first, so the episodes you've
worked on most recently are at the top. You can sort by **Service**, **Assigned
Staff**, **Start Date**, **Last Updated**, **Last Activity** or **Last Entry
Change**. Select that column's heading, then choose **Asc** or **Desc** from its
menu. Choosing the direction that is already ticked turns that sort off.
**Client**, **Status** and **Risk** can't be sorted on.

**Last Updated** and **Last Entry Change** are hidden when the list first opens.
To show them, use **View** above the table (on a wide screen). See
[Sorting <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> by recent activity](/releases/2026-09-09-care-journey-sorting)
for what each date means.

## Managing kaimahi

On the Overview tab, the staff card (**<Term path="caseWorker" plural /> assigned**) has a **Manage** button. It opens a window where you can:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Add Staff Member** | Assigns another <Term path="caseWorker" /> to the episode |
| **Make Primary** | Makes that person the primary responsible staff member |
| **Remove** | Unassigns a staff member, optionally with a reason |

Two rules apply when removing staff:

- The **primary responsible** staff member can't be removed directly — make someone else primary first.
- You can't remove the **last** remaining staff member — at least one must stay assigned.

## Changing status

From the episode's Overview tab you can change status manually, depending on where the episode currently sits:

| From | Action | To |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Awaiting Commencement** | **Start working with ...** (banner at the top) | **Active** |
| **Active** | Put on Hold | **On Hold** |
| **On Hold** | **Resume <Term path="serviceEpisode" />** | **Active** |

A service can choose not to allow **Put on Hold**. If yours does not, you see the message **On Hold is not enabled for this service.** See [Smaller changes](/releases/2026-11-09-smaller-changes#some-services-do-not-allow-on-hold).

<Callout color="amber" title="Once closed, an episode is read-only">
There's no manual action to move an episode to **Completed** or **Closed** from this page — that only happens through the closure process. See [Closing an Episode](/care-journeys/closing-an-episode).
</Callout>

## Recording activity

Work against the episode — visits, calls, case notes — is recorded on the **Activities** tab. See [Recording Activities](/activities/overview) for the full guide.
