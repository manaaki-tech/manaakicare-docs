# Closing a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />

Ending a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> — whether service was completed successfully or ended for another reason — starts from the **Actions** menu on the episode's Overview tab: choose **Exit <Term path="serviceEpisode" />**.

<Callout color="teal" title="You may be requesting rather than closing">
Each service lists the positions allowed to close directly — by default supervisors and managers. If yours isn't on that list, exiting submits a request for a manager to approve instead of closing the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> immediately, the dialog is titled **Request <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> Exit**, and you'll also pick an approver. The fields below are the same either way. See [Manager approval for <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> exits](/releases/2026-09-12-exit-approval).
</Callout>

<Callout color="amber" title="This cannot be undone">
Once an episode is Completed or Closed, its status is final — there's no action in the app to reopen it or change it further.
</Callout>

## Completion vs closure

The dialog uses a single **Closure Reason** field, but the reason you pick decides whether the episode ends as **Completed** or **Closed**:

| Closure Reason | Resulting status |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Service completed** | **Completed** |
| Anything else — released from prison, transferred to another service, moved out of area, left against advice, left without notice, left involuntarily, imprisoned, sanctioned by drug court, consent withdrawn, died, other, not stated | **Closed** |

Only "Service completed" counts as a true completion; every other reason closes the episode without it. Your organisation's administrator configures the exact list of reasons available, so yours may differ slightly from the list above.

Where a reason has an explanation attached to it, a small **i** icon sits beside its name in the dropdown — hover or focus it to read what that reason means. Not every reason has one; that's up to how your organisation has set them up.

These explanations are worth reading when two reasons sound similar. Picking the right one is what keeps your organisation's closure figures meaningful.

## What you provide

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Closure Reason** | Yes | Selects Completed vs Closed as above |
| **Exit Summary** | Yes | Free text. The form won't let you submit until you've written at least 10 characters. Replaces the old Exit Summary Document upload. |
| **End Date** | Yes | Cannot be after today |
| **Please specify** | Only if reason is "Other" | Free text |
| **Follow-up required** | No | If ticked, a **Follow-up date** is required (up to six months out) |

If any compliance requirements for the episode are still outstanding, they're listed as blockers and you'll need to enter a justification before the button at the bottom becomes available.

## Withdrawing consent as part of closure

If you select **Consent Withdrawn** as the closure reason, the dialog adds a dedicated section: a reason for the withdrawal, and a checkbox confirming the client has requested it. Both are required before you can proceed, and the button changes to **Complete & Withdraw Consent**.

<Callout color="amber" title="Withdrawing consent affects more than this episode">
Consent is withdrawn at the <Term path="referral" /> level, not just this episode. If the client has other open <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> under the same <Term path="referral" />, those are closed too — not just the one you're closing here.
</Callout>

## What happens automatically

Completing this dialog:

- Sets the episode's status to **Completed** or **Closed**, and records the end date and closure reason on it
- Sets the linked <Term path="referral" />'s status to match (**Completed** or **Closed**)
- Creates an "Exit Episode" activity recording the reason and what you wrote in the Exit Summary, visible on the episode's Activities tab. In that activity the summary is labelled **Closure Notes**, which is the field's older name

## After closing

Closed and completed episodes move off the **Active Care** page and onto **Completed Care**, where you can still open them to review their history — you just can't change them further.
