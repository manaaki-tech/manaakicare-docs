# Starting a <Term path="serviceEpisode" />

"Starting" a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is really two separate things: the episode being **created** (which happens automatically) and the episode being **commenced** (which is something a <Term path="caseWorker" /> does deliberately, once they're ready to begin). This page covers both.

## Creation is automatic

A <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created the moment a <Term path="referral" /> is accepted into a service — there's nothing to fill in beyond what's already been done in the dispatch or approval step. Which status it's created in depends on whether staff were assigned at that point:

| How the episode was created | Starting status |
|------------------------------|------------------|
| **Accept & assign kaimahi** at dispatch, or a supervisor approves and assigns a <Term path="caseWorker" /> in one step | **Awaiting Commencement** — staff is already assigned |
| **Accept entry** at dispatch, with no <Term path="caseWorker" /> chosen yet | **Awaiting Staff Assignment** |

See [Submitting for Review](/entries/submitting-for-review) for the dispatch and approval steps that trigger this.

## If the episode is Awaiting Staff Assignment

Open the episode and add a <Term path="caseWorker" /> from the **Assigned Staff** card.

<Callout color="amber" title="Tick 'Set as primary responsible' — it is not automatic">
The checkbox in the Add Staff Member dialog starts **unticked**, and nothing sets a primary for you, not even for the very first person you assign.

An episode with staff but no primary cannot be commenced: the button stays disabled. If you have assigned somebody and still cannot start the work, this is almost certainly why — open the dialog again and tick the box.
</Callout>

<Callout color="teal" title="Adding staff later works the same way">
This is the same **Add Staff Member** action described in [Managing Episodes](/care-journeys/managing-episodes) — assigning the first person to an unstaffed episode is what advances its status.
</Callout>

## Commencing the episode

Once a <Term path="caseWorker" /> is assigned, the episode sits in **Awaiting Commencement** until someone commences it. This is the actual "start" — it's what marks the beginning of active service delivery.

A banner on the episode's Overview tab says the service has not started yet, with a button reading **Start working with…** followed by the person's name. It is enabled once a primary responsible staff member is set; before that it stays disabled with a note that staff need to be assigned first.

Clicking it moves the episode to **Active** and:

- Sets the <Term path="referral" />'s status to **Service In Progress**
- Anchors the episode's compliance due dates to its start date

<Callout color="amber" title="Who can commence it">
There's no separate approval step — whoever can open the episode (typically the assigned <Term path="caseWorker" />, or a supervisor/programme manager for services they oversee) can commence it, provided a primary staff member is set.
</Callout>
