# Family Relationships

This page covers working with a whānau group's membership. For what a whānau group is, and what the **Primary contact** and **Service user** flags mean, see [whānau groups](/concepts/whanau-groups).

## Viewing a whānau group

The **Whānau** tab on a <Term path="serviceUser" />'s [details page](/whanau/viewing-client-details) lists everyone linked to their whānau group:

<Screen
  src="/img/clients/whanau-members.png"
  alt="The whanau tab listing family members with their phone, email and role"
  caption="One member is marked Primary. Phone and email are hidden until you click the eye beside them."
/>

| Column | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Name** | Linked to that person's own <Term path="serviceUser" /> record, if they are one (see below) |
| **Phone** / **Email** | Masked by default, click to reveal |
| **Role** | A **Primary** badge if this person is the primary contact for the group |
| **Actions** | New Referral, Remove, Edit |

Click **View Family Page** to open the whānau group on its own page, outside the context of any one member.

## Adding a member

Click **Add Member** on the Whānau tab. You can either:

- **Find Existing Person** — search by name, then set whether they're a service user and whether to sync them to Support Contacts, or
- **Create New Person** — enter first name and last name (required), plus date of birth, gender, phone, and email, with the same two role toggles

Creating a new person shows a warning if a similarly-named person already exists, so you can use **Find Existing Person** instead if it's a match.

## Starting a referral for a member

Click the **New Referral** icon next to a member to start a <Term path="referral" /> for them.

<Callout color="teal" title="Non-service-users are prompted first">
If the member isn't marked as a service user yet, you'll see a prompt — **"Register as service user?"** — before you can continue. Confirming marks them as a service user and takes you straight into the <Term path="referral" /> wizard with them pre-selected.
</Callout>

## Removing a member

Click the **Remove** icon and confirm. This unlinks the person from the whānau group — their own record is not deleted, and they can be added back later.

## Editing a member

Click the **Edit** icon to change the Primary contact and Keep in sync with Support Contacts flags for that member.
