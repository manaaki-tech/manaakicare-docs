# Whānau groups

Manaaki Central groups people into **whānau groups** so you can see who belongs
together and move between their records.

<Callout color="amber" title="A membership list, not a family tree">
A whānau group records *who* is in the group. It does **not** record how people
are related to one another — there is no parent, sibling or caregiver
relationship stored between two members.

If you are looking for where to record that somebody is a grandmother rather
than an aunt, it is not here. The nearest thing is the free-text relationship
field on a support network contact.
</Callout>

## The two flags on a member

Every member carries two flags, and the difference between them causes more
confusion than anything else in this part of the system:

- **Primary contact** — marks the main point of contact for the whānau group.
  One person per group.
- **Service user** — whether this person is themself receiving services, with
  their own record and <Term path="referral" /> history.

A member who is **not** a service user appears as plain text rather than a link,
because they have no record of their own to open yet. They can be promoted to one
later, and starting a <Term path="referral" /> for them will prompt exactly that.

There is a third toggle, **Keep in sync with Support Contacts**, available only
for non-service-user members. Turning it on also lists that person in the
<Term path="serviceUser" />'s support contacts, so you do not have to enter them
twice.

## Why a person can appear twice

Support contacts and whānau members are separate lists serving different
purposes: the whānau group is about who this person belongs with, the support
network is about who is involved in their care. Somebody can legitimately be in
both, which is what the sync toggle is for.
