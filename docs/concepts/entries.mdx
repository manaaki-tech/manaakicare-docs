# <Term path="referral" plural /> and <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />

A <Term path="referral" /> is how somebody enters service. It records who they
are, who referred them, what they need, and what consent is on file.

Once it is dispatched to a service and somebody is assigned, a
<Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created alongside it. That is the record of the
work itself.

<Callout color="teal" title="Two different records, not two names for one">
The <Term path="referral" /> is the **intake** record — the request for service.
The <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is the **delivery** record, created once a
<Term path="caseWorker" /> is allocated.

They have separate statuses and separate lifecycles, and both stay linked to the
person. This is the single most useful thing to understand about the system: when
somebody says "it's still showing as pending", the first question is *which of
the two records* they mean.
</Callout>

## The journey end to end

1. **Intake** — a staff member completes the three-step <Term path="referral" /> form
2. **Dispatch** — the <Term path="referral" /> is sent to a service, either accepted directly or sent to a manager for approval
3. **Allocation** — a <Term path="caseWorker" /> is assigned and a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created
4. **Delivery** — the <Term path="caseWorker" /> starts the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> and records <Term path="activity" plural />
5. **Closure** — the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is completed or closed

Each of those steps has its own how-to page. This page is only about what the
things *are*.

## Ways a <Term path="referral" /> can start {#ways-a-referral-can-start}

| Route | How |
|-------|-----|
| **New <Term path="referral" />** | The **+ New Entry** button in the header, or `/referrals/new` |
| **From a client** | The **New Entry** link on a client's whānau tab, which preselects that person |

## <Term path="referral" /> types {#referral-types}

The **referral type** determines who is recorded as the referrer:

| Type | Referrer is |
|------|-------------|
| **Self** | The person themselves |
| **Whānau** | A whānau member |
| **Educational** | A contact at a school or educational organisation |
| **Professional** | A contact at another organisation |

## Who can create one

The **+ New Entry** button appears for staff in an **intake** position, and for
**supervisors and programme managers**. If you are in a <Term path="caseWorker" />
position you will not see it — <Term path="referral" plural /> come to you already
allocated.

What happens after dispatch is also role-dependent: approving a service request
and allocating a <Term path="caseWorker" /> are supervisor and programme manager
actions.

<Callout color="teal" title="Based on your position, not just your role">
Visibility is worked out from the **positions you hold on your assigned
services**, falling back to your role if you have no service assignments. So two
people with the same job title can see different things if their service
positions differ.

If you think you should be able to create one and cannot, ask your administrator
to check your service positions — it is rarely a fault.
</Callout>

## Statuses

A <Term path="referral" /> moves through intake statuses, then into allocation and
delivery. Consent is tracked separately and can change at any point.

For the complete list with definitions, and the lifecycle diagrams, see [how the
work flows](/concepts/how-the-work-flows#status-reference).
