### <Term path="referral" /> Intake Steps {#referral-intake-steps}

The intake form is a three-step wizard. Each step must be completed before the next unlocks:

| Step | Label | What is captured |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| 1 | Intake | Service first, then the client, the referrer and the reason for the <Term path="referral" /> |
| 2 | Consent | How consent was obtained, and what was provided to the client |
| 3 | Review & Submit | Read-only confirmation, then submit and dispatch to a service |

The <Term path="referral" /> record is created at the **end of Step 1**, not at final submission — from that point it exists with a reference number and **Draft** status and can be returned to later.

Once all three steps are complete and the entry is submitted, it moves to **Pending Service Dispatch**.

For the full walkthrough see [Creating a Referral](/entries/creating-a-referral).

### Role Quick Reference

| Role | Primary Responsibilities | <Term path="dashboard" /> Focus |
|------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| **Intake Officer** | Search/create clients, complete 3-step intake, dispatch to services, can directly assign <Term path="caseWorker" plural /> | New <Term path="referral" plural />, review queue, service dispatch |
| **Head of Intake / Intake Officer** | Review all <Term path="referral" /> information and submit review | Review queue, pending reviews |
| **Supervisor / Program Manager** | Approve/reject service requests, select <Term path="caseWorker" plural /> on approval, monitor team | Pending service reviews, team <Term path="activity" /> |
| **<Term path="caseWorker" />** | Activate <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural />, deliver services, record activities, close <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> | Active <Term path="cases" />, follow-ups due, client interactions |

### Priority Guidelines

There are five priority levels. The <Term path="referral" /> form shows the expected response time against each one as you select it.

| Priority | Response time | When to Use | Example Scenarios |
|----------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Emergency** | Now | Immediate safety concern requiring emergency response | Child at risk, medical emergency |
| **Crisis** | 4 hours | Crisis situation, handle urgently | Acute family breakdown, imminent harm |
| **Urgent** | 24 hours | Needs attention within a day | Family in distress, time-sensitive support needed |
| **Priority** | 3 days | Important but not an emergency | New client intake, scheduled assessment overdue |
| **Routine** | 5 days | Standard processing | Regular check-in, administrative <Term path="referral" /> |

## Status Reference

All status values used across the system, grouped by lifecycle. These are the values you will see in badges, filters, and reports.

### <Term path="referral" /> statuses {#referral-statuses}

These statuses track where an entry is in the intake-to-allocation pipeline.

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Entry has been created — Step 1 complete, Consent not yet done |
| **Pending Consent** | Awaiting consent and agreement |
| **Pending Review** | Consent recorded, awaiting review and submission |
| **Pending Service Dispatch** | Submitted, ready for service selection and allocation |
| **Pending Service Review** | Service request sent to supervisor/programme manager for approval |
| **Accepted Pending Allocation** | Service accepted, <Term path="caseWorker" /> selection pending |
| **Allocated Awaiting Service** | <Term path="caseWorker" /> assigned, <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> created and ready to commence |
| **Service In Progress** | Service delivery underway |
| **Completed** | Service concluded successfully — final status |
| **Closed** | Entry closed without completing — final status |

**Completed** and **Closed** are the only final statuses. There is no separate "Cancelled" or "Withdrawn" status — an entry that is cancelled, or whose consent is withdrawn, ends up as **Closed** with a recorded reason.

### <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> statuses {#service-episode-statuses}

Once a <Term path="referral" /> is allocated, a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created and moves through its own lifecycle.

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Awaiting Staff Assignment** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> created, no <Term path="caseWorker" /> yet confirmed |
| **Awaiting Commencement** | <Term path="caseWorker" /> assigned, waiting for them to activate the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> |
| **Active / In Progress** | <Term path="caseWorker" /> has activated the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> — service delivery is underway |
| **On Hold** | Delivery temporarily paused (e.g. client unavailable, waiting on external factor) |
| **Completed** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> successfully concluded |
| **Closed** | <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> closed without full completion (early closure or termination) |

### Consent statuses

Consent is tracked separately from the <Term path="referral" /> status and may change at any point. It is not a single status field — consent is **active** when all of the following hold, and **withdrawn** once it has been withdrawn.

| State | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Consent active** | Consent to service and information sharing are both recorded, guardian consent is complete where required, consent has not expired, and it has not been withdrawn |
| **Consent withdrawn** | The client has withdrawn consent — service delivery must not proceed |

:::warning
Withdrawing consent is not reversible in the system, and it does more than change a flag: it **closes any active <Term path="serviceEpisode" plural /> and closes the <Term path="referral" /> itself**, recording the withdrawal reason. Check with your supervisor before withdrawing consent on anyone's record.
:::
