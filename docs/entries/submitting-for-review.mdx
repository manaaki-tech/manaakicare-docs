# Submitting and Dispatching a <Term path="referral" />

Once the Intake and Consent steps are complete, the final step is to review the <Term path="referral" />, submit it, and dispatch it to a service.

## Before you submit

The **Review & Submit** step shows everything you have entered, grouped by section. Each section has an **Edit** link that takes you back to the relevant step so you can correct it.

Check that:

- The client is the right person, and is not a duplicate of an existing record
- The service is correct — it determines who picks the <Term path="referral" /> up
- The urgency level reflects the actual need
- Consent is recorded, and any signed consent document is attached

## Submitting

Click **Submit Entry**. The <Term path="referral" /> moves to **Ready to Send** and the dispatch dialog opens automatically.

<Callout color="teal" title="You can dispatch later">
Submitting and dispatching are deliberately two separate steps. **Ready to Send** means "reviewed, but not yet sent to a team" — it is a normal place for a <Term path="referral" /> to sit, not an error.

If you close the dispatch dialog without choosing a service, nothing is lost. The <Term path="referral" /> stays on your dashboard with that status, and you can dispatch it whenever you are ready.
</Callout>

## Dispatching to a service

The service you chose in Step 1 is already filled in. Under **How should this be handled?**, choose one of three options.

| Option | What it does | Who it suits |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| **Accept entry** | Accepts the <Term path="referral" /> and creates the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />, without assigning anyone yet | You know which service, but not yet which <Term path="caseWorker" /> |
| **Accept & assign kaimahi** | Accepts and assigns a <Term path="caseWorker" /> in one action, creating the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> immediately | You have authority to allocate and know who is taking it |
| **Send for approval** | Sends a service request to a supervisor or Service Manager to approve and allocate | Allocation is someone else's decision |

<Callout color="amber" title="Signed consent document">
If consent was **Written** or **Electronic** and no signed consent form has been uploaded, the dispatch dialog shows **Signed consent form not uploaded**. It is a warning, not a block — you can still dispatch, but the document remains outstanding against the <Term path="referral" />.
</Callout>

After a successful dispatch you can return to your dashboard or open the <Term path="referral" /> to view it.

## Dispatching later

To pick up a <Term path="referral" /> you submitted but did not dispatch:

1. Find it on your dashboard under **In Review**, or in the <Term path="referral" plural /> list on the **Drafts** tab, with **Status** set to **Ready to Send**
2. Open it
3. Click **Send to Team**

## What happens next

### If you sent it for approval

The <Term path="referral" /> moves to **Pending Service Review** and appears in the supervisor's or Service Manager's queue. They can:

- **Accept** — they can choose a <Term path="caseWorker" /> in **Assign to Staff (optional)** and add notes. If they choose someone, a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created.
- **Reject** — they must give a reason. The <Term path="referral" /> returns to dispatch so it can be sent to a different service.

### If you accepted it directly

The <Term path="referral" /> is accepted straight away and a <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> is created, with no manager approval step.

### Then

The assigned <Term path="caseWorker" /> sees the <Term path="serviceEpisode" /> on their dashboard under **Ready to start**. They start it, and service delivery begins — from that point work is recorded as <Term path="activity" plural /> against the <Term path="serviceEpisode" />.
